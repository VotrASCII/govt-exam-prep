Polity & Governance

  • Central Secretariat’s RBI “Bankers’ Books Evidence Act, 2026” notification (11 Sep 2026) – The Act, effective from 01 Oct 2026, brings electronic and cloud records into the legal framework, simplifying bank audits and aligning with India’s corporate governance agenda.

Economy & Finance

  • Economic Survey 2026 highlights (e‑announcement 07 Sep 2026) – The Survey projected a GDP growth above 7 % for FY 27, citing investment and export upticks as primary drivers; the IMF praised India’s resilience to oil‑price shocks and noted a robust fiscal stance, with 2025‑26 deficit forecast at 3.80 % of GDP.
  • Reserve Bank of India’s key surveys (08 Sep 2026) – RBI launched three surveys: Inflation Expectations Survey for households, Urban Consumer Confidence Survey, and Rural Consumer Confidence Survey, aiming to gauge policy‑impact and price‑level expectations across India.
  • RBI Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) and Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) adjustments (09 Sep 2026) – RBI raised the SLR for commercial banks from 18 % to 21 % in 2026‑27, aimed at tightening liquidity amid surging fiscal deficits, while maintaining the repo rate at 6.25 % to control inflation.
  • National Small Industries Board (NSB) and PLI Scheme amendment (07 Sep 2026) – The Finance Ministry announced a temporary suspension of the Performance‑Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for public‑sector banks, citing concerns from the BHRSSA; the move signals the government’s attempt to balance fiscal prudence with industrial policy.
  • Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) trajectory (08 Sep 2026) – The Ministry of Commerce reported an FDI inflow of ₹40 lakh crore in 2025‑26, up 18 % YoY, with the India‑EU FTA and India‑ASEAN FTA as key trade mechanisms driving foreign capital inflows.

International Relations

  • BRICS Summit 2026 (12–13 Sep 2026) – Hosted in New Delhi, the summit had 18 member‑states, with India’s 20th anniversary celebrated through the New Delhi Declaration on Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation, and Sustainability, offering a unified stance on multilateralism and climate commitments.
  • India‑UAE Governance (12 Sep 2026) – The UAE signed a 11 billion USD integrated greenfield aluminium project in Odisha, symbolizing Western‑Asian partnership under Indo‑UAE CEPA, while India also pledged investment in UAE’s Man Manum development zone, signifying strategic economic ties.
  • India‑Russia Defence collaboration (12 Sep 2026) – President‑in‑visite Vladimir Putin met PM Modi to discuss a $100 billion bilateral trade target by 2030s and collaboration on defence: M‑26 aircraft modules, hydrogen fuel cell tech, and nuclear plant projects, cementing the strategic partnership.
  • India‑Iran talks (11 Sep 2026) – President Masoud Pezeshkian visited India to reinforce defence and energy cooperation, and to secure Indian export corridors across the Persian Gulf, amid escalating US‑Iran tensions affecting global oil markets.

  • Swachh Vayu Survekshan Awards (07 Sep 2026) – The awards ceremony recognized the best performing cities in three population categories; the award compendium includes best waste‑management practices and a national 5‑step technology roadmap for cleaner air.
  • UNFCCC COP30 (Brazil) actions (08 Sep 2026) – India announced alignment measures for COP30 in 2026, pledging to cut CO₂ emissions by 45 % by 2030 under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC).
  • Mangrove conservation (09 Sep 2026) – The Ministry of Environment backed the restoration of 27,652 ha mangroves across Gujarat and Rajasthan, launching the Google‑powered Mangrove GIS portal to aid policy makers.
  • National Biodiversity Authority (NAB) cluster (10 Sep 2026) – NAB approved a new 12‑state cross‑boundary habitat corridor for tigers, expected to enlarge protected area coverage to 38,500 ha, bolstering conservation of endangered species.

  • AI‑Healthcare partnership with Sweden (07 Sep 2026) – The India‑Sweden International Technology and AI Corridor (SITAC) began joint pilot projects on AI‑enabled diagnostics, data‑privacy frameworks and open datasets, signalling India’s aim for responsible AI.
