Polity & Governance

  • India’s Cabinet approves Integrated Transport & Logistics Authority (ITLA). The nodal ministry, Ministry of Commerce and Industry, aims to unify planning for roads, rail, ports and logistics parks, cutting redundancies and lowering logistics costs to boost competitiveness.
  • Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman meets Singapore’s President and Prime Minister. Discussions focused on strengthening economic ties, green growth and digital payments, signalling India’s intent to expand trade and investment with the region’s largest economy.
  • President of the Republic of Indonesia announces new mandatory halal certification rules. Effective 17 October, these requirements will raise compliance costs for Indian exporters of food, cosmetics, medicines and medical devices, impacting the import‑export cost structure.
  • India joins a joint ministerial statement with 14 other economies on excess capacity. Together they agreed to tackle structural overcapacity in sectors like EVs, batteries and semiconductors, reflecting India’s role in multilateral trade governance.
  • 8th Pay Commission launches consultations in Bengaluru. Updates on fitment factors, salary levels, allowances and pension of central government employees will shape remuneration policy for the public sector.
  • Gujarat Institute of Development Studies study on Lakhpati Didi. The flagship women‑empowerment programme, launched in 2023, claims a 61 % rise in household income and a 48 % increase in women‑led enterprises, underscoring the socioeconomic impact of targeted schemes.
