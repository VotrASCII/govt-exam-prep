RBI & Monetary Policy

  • ★ The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) absorbed surplus liquidity through four Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auctions under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF), all clearing at a 5.49 per cent cut-off. A 3-day VRRR on 12 October 2026 had ₹2,00,000 crore notified and ₹37,558 crore accepted. Overnight auctions followed on 13 October (₹1,50,000 crore notified, ₹71,580 crore accepted), 14 October (₹50,000 crore notified, ₹25,533 crore accepted) and 15 October (₹50,000 crore notified, ₹9,562 crore accepted). Offers fell short of the notified amount every time and were fully accepted, which points to a system with surplus liquidity.
  • ★ No Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) decision appears in the Week 46 releases, so do not infer any repo-rate change from this data. The only rate signal this week is the 5.49 per cent VRRR cut-off. For revision, the framework is flexible inflation targeting at 4 per cent with a ±2 per cent tolerance band. The target is treated as missed if inflation stays above the 6 per cent upper tolerance level for three consecutive quarters.
  • The liquidity table in the Weekly Statistical Supplement (5–11 October 2026) shows net absorption on every day. On 7 October the VRRR absorbed ₹2,55,827 crore and the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) ₹1,72,267 crore, against only ₹500 crore taken through the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF). Net absorption that day was ₹4,27,594 crore, the highest in the table, and it fell to ₹1,38,979 crore on 10 October. The pattern confirms that banks were parking funds with RBI rather than borrowing from it.
  • ★ Foreign exchange reserves stood at US$726,027 million (₹70,22,417 crore) on 9 October 2026, down US$8,578 million over the week. Foreign Currency Assets (FCA) were US$597,605 million, gold US$105,094 million, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) US$18,548 million and the Reserve Position in the International Monetary Fund (IMF) US$4,780 million. The data comes from the RBI Bulletin's Weekly Statistical Supplement released on 16 October 2026. Gold and FCA are valued at market prices, so they move with valuation as well as with RBI transactions.
  • RBI issued Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on the Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR) on 15 October 2026. The FAQs clarify the FERR requirements introduced by A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 26 dated 10 October 2026 and Circular No. 27 dated 12 October 2026. A.P. (DIR Series) stands for Authorised Persons (Directions) Series, the circular line used for foreign exchange rules. The FAQs build on the earlier circulars and add no new requirement.
  • ★ The Government Security (GS) auction of 6.94% GS 2036 for ₹34,000 crore was held on 16 October 2026. The security matures on 11 May 2036 and was sold by the multiple-price method, with settlement on 19 October. Competitive bids of ₹94,072.116 crore arrived across 400 bids, and the cut-off price was ₹96.91 (Yield to Maturity, YTM, 7.3955 per cent). The Weighted Average Yield (WAY) was 7.3925 per cent. Partial allotment was 20.7188 per cent, non-competitive bids of ₹39.291 crore were fully accepted, and devolvement on Primary Dealers (PDs) was NIL.
  • The underwriting auction for the 6.94% GS 2036 set the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) commission cut-off at 0.38 paise per ₹100. The Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) was ₹17,010 crore in total and ₹810 crore per PD. ACU bids of ₹16,990 crore were accepted, so the full ₹34,000 crore was underwritten. Bids were placed on e-Kuber, RBI's Core Banking Solution, between 9:00 and 9:30 a.m. under the 2007 underwriting scheme. The Government of India (GoI) had also kept the option to retain up to ₹2,000 crore of additional subscription.
  • ★ Treasury Bill (T-Bill) auction results of 14 October 2026 showed rising yields with tenor. The notified amounts were ₹8,000 crore (91-day), ₹8,000 crore (182-day) and ₹7,000 crore (364-day). Cut-off yields were 5.7281 per cent, 6.2896 per cent and 6.4891 per cent respectively, with cut-off prices of ₹98.5920, ₹96.9592 and ₹93.9220. The next auction, announced on 16 October for 21 October 2026, offers ₹23,000 crore in total (₹8,000 crore, ₹8,000 crore and ₹7,000 crore) using the multiple-price method. Retail investors can bid non-competitively up to 5 per cent of the notified amount, with settlement on 22 October.
  • RBI's Open Market Operation (OMO) sale on 13 October 2026 drained liquidity. RBI notified an aggregate ₹25,000 crore, received bids of ₹55,518 crore and accepted ₹25,000 crore. Acceptances were 7.88% GS 2030 (₹2,820 crore), 6.10% GS 2031 (₹8,430 crore), 7.95% GS 2032 (₹1,600 crore), 7.26% GS 2033 (₹2,735 crore) and 7.18% GS 2033 (₹9,415 crore). No bid was accepted in 7.10% GS 2034. Cut-off yields ranged from 7.1428 to 7.3947 per cent, and settlement was on 14 October.
  • A conversion/switch auction of ₹20,000 crore was announced for 19 October 2026. The source securities are 8.26% GS 2027, 7.17% GS 2028, 7.06% GS 2028, 7.26% GS 2029, 6.03% GS 2029 and 6.79% GS 2029. The destination securities are 7.19% GS 2060, 7.41% GS 2036, 7.23% GS 2039, 6.64% GS 2035, 7.50% GS 2034 and 7.57% GS 2033. Source security price must equal the Financial Benchmarks India Pvt. Ltd. (FBIL) closing price of the previous working day. The auction is multiple-price, with settlement on 21 October.
  • ★ State Government Securities (SGS) auctions: ₹31,200 crore was notified on 13 October 2026 and ₹30,005 crore accepted. Assam accepted only ₹805 crore in its 11-year paper and Gujarat accepted nothing in its 12-year paper. The highest cut-off was Tamil Nadu's 25-year paper at 8.21 per cent and the lowest was the Odisha SGS 2032 re-issue at 7.8520 per cent. A further ₹24,400 crore auction across 11 states is set for 19 October 2026 on e-Kuber. SGS investments count as eligible Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) investments under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
  • ★ RBI launched the 116th round of the Industrial Outlook Survey (IOS) and the 51st round of the Services and Infrastructure Outlook Survey (SIOS) for Q3:2026-27 (October–December 2026). Both surveys gauge current-quarter conditions and expectations for the next quarter, plus the following two quarters (Q1 and Q2 of 2027-28). They cover demand, financial conditions, employment and price situation, and results are published in summary form without identifying respondents. Genesis Management & Market Research Pvt. Ltd. conducts them for RBI's Division of Enterprise Surveys.
  • RBI released money supply (M3) data for the fortnight ended 30 September 2026. M3 stood at ₹3,37,66,475 crore, up 2.1 per cent over the fortnight and 16.9 per cent year-on-year. Time deposits were ₹2,55,41,542 crore, currency with the public ₹42,07,251 crore, and demand deposits rose 20.9 per cent year-on-year. Net bank credit to government was ₹94,15,530 crore, while bank credit to the commercial sector was ₹2,38,49,938 crore (up 18.8 per cent year-on-year).
  • ★ Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) premature redemption prices were announced for two tranches. SGB 2019-20 Series V (issued 15 October 2019) was redeemable on 15 October 2026 at ₹14,725 per unit, based on 12–14 October prices. SGB 2020-21 Series VII (issued 20 October 2020) was due 19 October 2026, because 20 October is a holiday, at ₹14,614 per unit, based on 14–16 October prices. Premature redemption is allowed only after the fifth year, on an interest payment date. The price is the simple average of the closing price of 999-purity gold over the previous three business days, published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA).

Banking & Financial Sector

  • ★ RBI sanctioned the amalgamation of the Indian branches of Emirates NBD Bank P.J.S.C. with RBL Bank Limited. The Scheme was sanctioned on 16 October 2026 under Section 44A(4) of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, with Emirates NBD as transferor and RBL Bank as transferee. It takes effect on 1 December 2026, when all Indian branches of Emirates NBD become branches of RBL Bank. This is a foreign bank's branch network being absorbed by a private-sector Indian bank.
  • ★ The Union Cabinet cleared the strategic sale of 60.72 per cent of IDBI Bank. The stake is split between GoI (30.48 per cent) and Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC, 30.24 per cent). The buyer is M/s 10955230 Canada Inc., a vehicle of Fairfax Financial Holdings Ltd., for ₹52,878.0806181 crore. After the sale GoI keeps 15 per cent and LIC 19 per cent, and both exit the Board. The release calls it the largest Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in India's financial sector to date.
  • IDBI's valuation was benchmarked by two independent agencies, one using Market Multiple and one Asset Valuation. The average benchmark share price was ₹75.99, and the winning bid was ₹81 per share, above that benchmark. A 17 October 2026 clarification noted that an attempt in 2003 to restrict dilution in the IDBI (Repeal) Act was rejected. It also cited the Delhi High Court's 17 December 2018 judgment, which found no prohibition on GoI diluting its stake. The disinvestment was announced in the Budget speeches of 2016-17 and 2021-22.
  • ★ The Scheduled Banks' Statement of Position as on 30 September 2026 gives the banking system's size. There were 120 Scheduled Commercial Banks, 26 Scheduled State Co-operative Banks and 55 Scheduled Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks. Scheduled Commercial Banks' deposits (other than from banks) were ₹2,83,95,693 crore and bank credit ₹2,29,94,658 crore. Food credit of Scheduled Commercial Banks was ₹96,286.36 crore, against ₹1,09,721.49 crore on 15 September 2026. The Urban Co-operative Bank (UCB) count is a recurring exam fact.
  • ★ Weekly Statistical Supplement data show strong credit and deposit growth. As on 30 September 2026, aggregate deposits of Scheduled Commercial Banks rose 17.8 per cent year-on-year and bank credit 19.4 per cent. Financial-year-to-date growth was 8.3 per cent for deposits and 7.7 per cent for credit. Under the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025, a fortnight is now defined as the 15th and last calendar day of a month, effective 15 December 2025, replacing alternate Fridays.
  • ★ Bankers' Conclave 3.0 was held at Mizoram University, Aizawl on 13 October 2026. It is a follow-up to Conclave 2.0 of December 2024, and the Northeast earlier received about ₹40,000 crore of investment commitments after the May 2025 summit. Union Minister Jyotiraditya M. Scindia (Development of North Eastern Region, DoNER) led the event, with participation from RBI, public sector banks, NABARD, SIDBI, India Post Payments Bank (IPPB), NEDFi and others. The Northeast's credit-deposit ratio is only about ₹47 of credit per ₹100 of deposits, and the conclave pressed banks for higher lending targets.
  • The conclave reported expansion and digital figures for the Northeast. There were 131 new bank branches and 126 new ATMs, and Unified Payments Interface (UPI) accounted for 90.2 per cent of all non-branch transactions. The earlier roadmap relaxed the population norm for opening a branch to 2,000 on a cluster basis, against 3,000 nationally. Action items included Business Correspondents, BC-Sakhis and Aadhaar-seeded CASA accounts where full branches are unviable.
  • ★ The Multi-State Cooperative Societies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2026 (MSCARO 2026) was notified. It brings a risk-based, Companies (Auditor's Report) Order (CARO)-style audit report for multi-state cooperative societies, with mandatory reporting of Non-Performing Assets (NPAs), overdue loans, liquidity, deposits, advances and borrowings. It also covers governance checks, cybersecurity and Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) compliance. It links to the Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies (CRCS) portal for dashboards and risk scoring. Implementation is phased, starting with societies above ₹1,000 crore.
  • ★ Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman's four-day Singapore visit (8–11 October 2026) focused on capital flows and digital finance. She met the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) on asset tokenisation, digital money and Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC), and MAS agreed to share its tokenisation experience. She urged investors such as Temasek, GIC and global funds to use the National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF) and the Gujarat International Finance Tec-City International Financial Services Centre (GIFT IFSC). The 4th India–Singapore Ministerial Roundtable (ISMR) was also reviewed.
  • The Competition Commission of India (CCI) approved three acquisitions on 14 October 2026. GRT Jewellers (India) Pvt. Ltd. gets a majority stake in Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri Ltd. NuageLux BidCo S.à r.l. gets about 23 per cent in Kids Clinic India Ltd. A group of Impact Fund Denmark, Belgian Investment Company for Developing Countries and Orios Fund III gets a shareholding in FarMart Service Pvt. Ltd.

Government Schemes & Budget

  • ★ The Prime Minister Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana (PMDDKY) completed one year on 11 October 2026. It was launched on 11 October 2025 for 100 districts, converging 36 Central schemes under 11 ministries, with district plans assessed on 121 indicators. Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan named Gaya (Bihar), Nagaur (Rajasthan) and Umaria (Madhya Pradesh) as the top three districts, and 18 districts were rewarded for six key objectives. A 200-day Khet Bachao Abhiyan will run from 22 October to 30 November 2026.
  • ★ The Cabinet approved continuation of the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) from 2026-27 to 2030-31 with an outlay of ₹53,428 crore. The scheme is renamed Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana, with sub-schemes renamed too (for example, Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme to PM Vridhavastha Pension Yojana). The disability benchmark for the Divyangjan pension falls to 40 per cent from 80 per cent, and the minimum age criterion is "since birth". Rates are ₹375 for ages 0–79 and ₹625 for 80 and above, and 3.20 crore beneficiaries are covered across 36 States/Union Territories (UTs).
  • ★ The Cabinet approved a Productivity Linked Bonus (PLB) of 78 days' wages for 11,40,770 railway employees. The total liability is ₹2,009.61 crore, with a maximum of ₹17,951 per employee. Eligible staff include track maintainers, loco pilots, train managers, station masters, supervisors and technicians. The justification cited 2025-26 performance of 1,670 million tonnes of cargo and about 7.41 billion passengers.
  • ★ Pradhan Mantri SETU (PM-SETU) saw its largest industry commitment so far. The scheme has a ₹60,000 crore outlay to upgrade 1,000 Government Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) and strengthen 5 National Skill Training Institutes (NSTIs). Shareholders' agreements were signed with ArcelorMittal-Nippon Steel India (AM/NS India) for 5 ITIs in Surat, Gujarat (₹240 crore) and 4 ITIs in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh (₹200 crore), totalling ₹440 crore. AM/NS India holds 51 per cent of each Special Purpose Vehicle, GoI 24.5 per cent and the state 24.5 per cent.
  • ★ BHAVYA is a plug-and-play industrial parks scheme with a ₹33,660 crore outlay. It aims at 100 parks of 25 to 1,000 acres each over six years (FY 2026-27 to FY 2031-32), with support up to ₹1 crore per acre. The first 50 parks are to be selected, and the portal went live in June 2026. A chemical-sector variant, BHAVYA-Rasayan, was approved in July 2026 for three chemical parks.
  • ★ The ₹1 lakh crore Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Scheme, launched in November 2025, widened its scope. From 8 October 2026 it also covers Advanced Chemicals, Petrochemicals and Material Technologies. Separately, 19 Second Level Fund Managers (14 Alternative Investment Funds and 5 other fund-routing organisations) were approved in the first cohort, with RDI Fund commitments of ₹7,131 crore. Funds must mobilise private capital equal to the RDI commitment.
  • PM GatiShakti's National Master Plan completed five years since its 13 October 2021 launch. It covers 58 Central Ministries and Departments and all 36 States/UTs. It integrates seven infrastructure engines: roads, railways, ports, waterways, airports, mass transport and logistics. The Network Planning Group has evaluated over 400 projects worth ₹20.23 lakh crore. The Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority (ITLA) prepares a 10-year National Transport Master Plan.
  • ★ Union Budget figures were cited at a Karnataka event. A release on the Shilp Samagam Mela said ₹1.98 lakh crore is allocated to social welfare schemes in the 2026-27 Budget, against a total Union Budget of ₹57 lakh crore. The same release said the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana has served about 4 crore beneficiaries. Treat these as figures "as cited" in the release.
  • Prime Minister Modi inaugurated projects worth ₹3,350 crore in Ladakh on 15 October 2026. They include a new Leh airport terminal, the 298-km Nimmu–Padum–Darcha road (₹2,500 crore, built by the Border Roads Organisation), the Hanu hydroelectric project with a transmission line, and Project Amrit Him Sarovar (40 reservoirs). A Petroglyph Conservation Park at Leh was also opened. A High Court Bench for Ladakh was approved in August 2026.
  • DoNER projects in Mizoram total ₹331 crore. Union Minister Scindia dedicated ₹102 crore of projects and laid foundation stones for ₹228.54 crore. The projects include a 74-km, 132 kV Bairabi–West Phaileng transmission line (₹54.95 crore) and the Pukzing–Silsuri road (₹100.24 crore). The Ministry's FY 2026-27 allocation is ₹6,812.30 crore.
  • The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) capped trade margins at 30 per cent of Maximum Retail Price (MRP) on 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs. The cap takes effect on 9 November 2026 under Paragraph 19 of the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013. MRPs may fall by up to 70 per cent, building on a 2019 pilot of 42 drugs. Manufacturers get a 7-day notice window, and prices appear on the Pharma Sahi Daam app.
  • The National Land Monetisation Corporation (NLMC) e-auction of Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL) land fetched ₹366.33 crore on day one. It sold 150 plots against a reserve of ₹312.48 crore, an average premium of 17.23 per cent, with a high above ₹1.80 lakh per square yard. A second phase is set for 30 October 2026 on RailTel's E-Nivida platform, and this fits the asset monetisation theme.
  • Special Campaign 6.0 for cleanliness and pendency disposal runs from 2 to 31 October 2026. Ministries, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) and Departments report scrap and e-waste disposal. The Department of Posts reported 73,814 cleanliness sites and ₹67.56 lakh from scrap by 16 October. The Railways' targets are 1 lakh sites, 7 lakh sq ft cleared and ₹600 crore from scrap.

Economy & Trade

  • ★ India's merchandise and services exports grew 13.84 per cent in April–September 2026-27. Total exports were US$476.20 billion against US$418.31 billion a year earlier. Merchandise exports were US$259.89 billion (up 18.51 per cent) and services exports US$216.31 billion (up 8.69 per cent). Merchandise imports were US$437.53 billion and services imports US$113.60 billion, and the merchandise trade deficit was US$177.64 billion.
  • September 2026 trade data show a widening gap. Total exports were US$80.75 billion (up 10.99 per cent) and imports US$94.05 billion (up 8.19 per cent), leaving a trade balance of minus US$13.30 billion. Fastest-growing merchandise exports were electronic goods (70.44 per cent), other cereals (49.88 per cent), handicrafts excluding hand-made carpet (43.19 per cent) and petroleum products (31.90 per cent). Gold imports fell 81.27 per cent and silver 49.11 per cent.
  • ★ The Wholesale Price Index (WPI) is now on a 2022-23 base year. The provisional September 2026 index was 112.5, with year-on-year inflation of 11.39 per cent, against 9.92 per cent in August (final index 110.8) and 9.78 per cent in July. Fuel and Power inflation was 25.85 per cent, Manufactured Products 9.76 per cent and Primary Articles 8.67 per cent. The Output Producer Price Index (OPPI) and the trial Input Producer Price Index (IPPI) were released at 112.5 and 106.1. The next release is on 16 November 2026.
  • ★ The September 2026 monthly Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) shows a stable labour market. The Labour Force Participation Rate (LFPR, age 15+) was 55.5 per cent, the female LFPR 34.6 per cent and the Worker Population Ratio 52.7 per cent. Overall unemployment was 5.0 per cent and rural unemployment 3.9 per cent, the lowest of 2026. The sample covered 3,70,513 persons. A separate backgrounder noted women's LFPR rising from 23.3 per cent (2017-18) to 40.0 per cent (2025).
  • Preliminary balance of payments data for August 2026 show a smaller current account deficit. The Current Account deficit was US$5.2 billion against US$6.1 billion a year earlier, with a merchandise deficit of US$26.6 billion (exports US$44.7 billion, imports US$71.3 billion). The services surplus was US$16.5 billion and net transfers US$12.8 billion. The Capital Account was US$50.6 billion, producing an overall surplus of US$45.4 billion. For April–August 2026 the current account deficit was US$18.3 billion against US$12.8 billion.
  • ★ Prime Minister Modi cited growth and energy figures at the Bloomberg New Economy Forum. He said the economy grew 7.8 per cent in April–June 2026 and that renewable capacity rose from 75 GW in 2014 to about 300 GW in 2026. India met its Paris Agreement target of 40 per cent non-fossil electricity capacity in November 2021, nine years early. Another release put non-fossil capacity at 308 GW of 558 GW total installed.
  • ★ India–United Kingdom trade: Piyush Goyal wants bilateral trade doubled by 2030 under the Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA). The 16th Joint Economic and Trade Committee (JETCO) joint statement noted 50 consignments cleared from India to the UK in the first 24 hours after CETA. The first shipments included Joha rice from Assam, soybean, light-weight vehicles and Scotch whisky. The 17th JETCO will be held in the UK in 2027, and priority areas include clean energy, advanced manufacturing, technology, financial services and healthcare.
  • A "Leveraging FTAs" outreach in Jaipur (17 October 2026) highlighted agreements with the UAE, Australia, EFTA states and the UK. Rajasthan aims to become a US$350 billion economy by 2029 and US$4.3 trillion by 2047. Its export target is ₹1.5 lakh crore by 2029. The India–Brunei Third Joint Trade Committee (14 October) also discussed pharmaceuticals, healthcare, energy and tourism.
  • Indian Railways loaded 135.35 million tonnes of freight in September 2026. This is 6.14 per cent above 127.52 million tonnes in September 2025, with food grain up 25 per cent, clinker 20 per cent and coal 9 per cent. Coal rakes supplied rose to 443 a day from 397, and freight revenue grew ₹1,266 crore (9.3 per cent). Cumulative April–September loading was 833.7 million tonnes, up 4.1 per cent.
  • Coal stocks at power plants have stayed above 18 million tonnes since 29 September 2026. Stocks were 18.932 million tonnes on 29 September and 18.205 million tonnes on 10 October. Daily supply averaged 2.49 million tonnes in 1–10 October against a plan of 2.37 million tonnes. The Ministry of Coal also noted 469 rakes loaded per day, against 426 in September.
  • ★ The Telecom Performance Indicator Report for the quarter ended 30 June 2026 shows continued growth. Total telephone subscribers were 1,348.08 million, internet subscribers 1,114.17 million and broadband subscribers 1,087.50 million. Overall tele-density was 94.31 per cent (urban 153.43 per cent, rural 60.89 per cent), against 93.26 per cent on 1 April 2026. The report also lists 915 permitted private satellite TV channels and 4 pay-DTH operators.
  • Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) signals a new data architecture. Dr P. K. Mishra's keynote referred to the New National Accounts Series with a 2022-23 base year, along with updated Consumer Price Index (CPI) and Index of Industrial Production (IIP) series. The India Post, Customs and railway releases are less relevant for exams than these base-year changes. Also note the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) meeting on "GST Reforms 3.0" in Chennai on 12 October, tied to the 57th GST Council recommendations.
  • The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) launched a Silver Refinery Scheme for digital hallmarking of silver bullion and coins. It covers three purity grades: 999.9, 999.5 and 999.0 parts per thousand. Governing standards include IS 2112:2025 for silver. This extends hallmarking, earlier used for gold, to silver.

International Affairs & Organizations

  • ★ The first BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting was held in New Delhi on 13 October 2026 under Home Minister Amit Shah. BIMSTEC stands for Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation. Home Ministers of Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Thailand adopted the Delhi Declaration. It reaffirmed zero tolerance for terrorism and covered drug control, cyber-crime, mutual legal assistance, extradition and disaster management. Myanmar and Thailand are the members that overlap with the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN).
  • ★ India holds the BRICS Presidency in 2026, and the BRICS Theatre Festival ran from 12 to 14 October 2026 at the National School of Drama. The BRICS theme is "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability", and the festival theme was "Shared Stories, Shared Futures". Listed participants were India, Thailand, Indonesia, Russia, Iran, Vietnam, South Africa, Kazakhstan and Ethiopia. The Ministry of Culture organised it, with Gajendra Singh Shekhawat as chief guest.
  • ★ India became a Contracting Party to the Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement (SIOFA), up from Cooperating Non-Contracting Party status. Indian-flagged vessels can now fish squids, toothfish and alfonsino in SIOFA waters. India gains access to scientific data and a role in the Scientific and Compliance Committees. SIOFA excludes highly migratory species, and India has been a contracting party of the Indian Ocean Tuna Commission since 1996.
  • ★ The Indian Navy handed over command of Combined Task Force 154 (CTF 154) on 14 October 2026 in Manama, Bahrain. It was the first Indian Navy contingent to lead a Combined Maritime Forces (CMF) task force, having assumed command on 11 February 2026. Its tenure included Operation Compass Rose 26-1, Operation Southern Readiness 26-2 and SPAR 2026 (5–6 October, 135 participants from 25 countries). In total it delivered over 7,800 training hours, with 39 countries represented.

Brazil sent BNS Barroso with a Lynx helicopter, while South Africa fielded SAS Amatola, SAS King Sekhukhune I, SAS Adam Kok III and the submarine SAS Manthatisi. The theme is "Stronger Together Towards a Shared Future in the Maritime Domain".

The Air Force marked its 94th anniversary, and a Centre for Air Power Studies seminar was held on 9 October.
  • India and the United Kingdom deepened ties beyond trade. INS Trishul and HMS Sutherland took part in Exercise KONKAN-26 (8–15 October 2026) off Plymouth. Sports / Environment / Science & Tech

  • ★ India won 85 medals at the 2026 Asian Games: 21 gold, 27 silver and 37 bronze. Services athletes (92 athletes across 19 disciplines) won 27 medals (6 gold, 11 silver, 10 bronze), which is 32 per cent of India's tally and 28.57 per cent of its golds. The announcement came on 15 October 2026 from the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. Ramsar sites are wetlands of international importance under the Ramsar Convention.
  • The International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA) inaugurated its new headquarters at 55 Lodhi Estate, New Delhi on 12 October 2026. Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav did the honours. As of that date, 29 countries had joined, 5 were observers and 11 more had consented to join. India's initial contribution is ₹150 crore over five years (2023-24 to 2027-28).

About 3.54 lakh machines were distributed between 2018-19 and 2025-26, and 26,109 new machines are planned for FY 2026-27. Funding across states totals ₹4,266.47 crore (Punjab ₹2,056.50 crore, Haryana ₹1,171.71 crore). Fire incidents from 15 September to 12 October 2026 fell 53.3 per cent in Haryana and 32 per cent in Uttar Pradesh.
  • The Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM) intensified Delhi-NCR enforcement. A review of 25 September to 9 October 2026 covered 124 inspections and found 55 violations. Cumulatively, as of 16 October, 28,868 units had been inspected, with 1,837 closure directions and 1,491 resumption orders. In Gurugram on 14 October, CAQM flagged 258 violations over 104 km of roads.

It was launched at the biannual Pre-Cyclone Exercise Meeting (October–December 2026) chaired by Dr Mrutyunjay Mohapatra, Director General of Meteorology.
