  • RBI Payment System updates such as NEFT, RTGS, UPI, RBI‑backed CBDC (Digital Rupee) or TIPS are absent from this week's announcements.
  • The RBI Annual Report statistics for FY 2025–26 (GNPA, NPA ratios, GDP forecasts) are not quoted in the Week 46 releases.
