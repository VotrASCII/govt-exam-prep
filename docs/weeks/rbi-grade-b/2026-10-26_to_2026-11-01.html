01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • ★ Programmable Central Bank Digital Currency (P-CBDC) was pushed for State Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT). At the 36th Conference of State Finance Secretaries on October 27, 2026 at the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Mumbai, Governor Sanjay Malhotra urged States to use P-CBDC, the programmable form of the Digital Rupee, for DBT. He also asked States to onboard the Unified Lending Interface (ULI) and make effective use of the State Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC) forum. This shows the Digital Rupee being positioned for government payments, so expect it in questions on Digital Rupee updates.
  • The 36th Conference of State Finance Secretaries had a fiscal-sustainability theme. The theme was 'Role of Quality of Expenditure, Effective Cash and Prudent Debt Management in Enhancing States' Fiscal Sustainability'. Finance Secretaries of 27 States and three Union Territories attended, along with officials of the Ministry of Finance, the Controller General of Accounts and the Comptroller and Auditor General of India. The Governor released a booklet, 'Compendium on Cash and Debt Management for State Governments', and the meeting reviewed State market borrowings, short-term financial accommodation from RBI and the Benchmark Issuance Strategy.
  • The 625th meeting of the RBI Central Board was held in Mumbai on October 30, 2026. Governor Sanjay Malhotra chaired it, and the Board took the Integrity Pledge for Vigilance Awareness Week 2026. It reviewed the economic outlook, the activities of the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) and select Central Office Departments. Deputy Governors Swaminathan J., Dr. Poonam Gupta, Shirish Chandra Murmu and Rohit Jain attended, as did Ms. Anuradha Thakur (Secretary, Department of Economic Affairs), Anand Gopal Mahindra, Janmejaya Kumar Sinha, Syed Akbaruddin and Ms. Annie George Mathew.
  • Premature redemption prices were announced for three Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) tranches. The redemption prices were ₹14,159 for SGB 2020-21 Series I (due October 28, 2026), ₹14,213 for SGB 2019-20 Series VI (due October 30, 2026) and ₹14,167 for SGB 2021-22 Series VII (due November 02, 2026). Premature redemption is allowed only after the fifth year from issue, on an interest payment date. The price is the simple average of the closing price of 999-purity gold of the previous three business days, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA).
  • Government of India dated securities worth ₹33,000 crore were auctioned on October 30, 2026. The four re-issued securities were 6.20% GS 2029 (₹9,000 crore), 6.57% GS 2033 (₹12,000 crore), 7.63% GS 2056 (₹9,000 crore) and 7.50% GOI SGrB 2056 (₹3,000 crore). Cut-off yields were 7.1218%, 7.3818%, 7.8459% and 7.8292% respectively, and there was no devolvement on Primary Dealers (PDs). The auction used the multiple price method on the RBI Core Banking Solution (e-Kuber), with settlement on November 02, 2026, and re-issued securities are auctioned on a price basis.
  • The underwriting auction set the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) commission for PDs. The cut-off rates, in paise per ₹100, were 0.92 for 6.20% GS 2029, 0.64 for 6.57% GS 2033, 0.74 for 7.63% GS 2056 and 1.00 for 7.50% GOI SGrB 2056. The Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) per PD was ₹215 crore, ₹286 crore, ₹215 crore and ₹72 crore for the four securities. These details come from the underwriting scheme notified on November 14, 2007.
  • The interest rate on the Government of India Floating Rate Bond (FRB) 2034 was fixed at 7.26% per annum. It applies to the half-year October 30, 2026 to April 29, 2027. The coupon is the average Weighted Average Yield (WAY) of the last three 182-day Treasury Bill (T-Bill) auctions plus a fixed spread of 0.98%.
  • The T-Bill auction results of October 28, 2026 and the next auction were announced. Cut-off yields were 5.9337% (91-day), 6.5493% (182-day) and 6.6900% (364-day), with WAYs of 5.9094%, 6.5140% and 6.6391%. The next auction is for ₹23,000 crore in total (₹8,000 crore, ₹8,000 crore and ₹7,000 crore), to be held on November 04, 2026 with settlement on November 05. It uses the multiple price method, and retail non-competitive allocation is limited to 5% of the notified amount.
  • State Government Securities (SGS) auction results of October 27, 2026 showed wide State-wise variation. Of ₹23,300 crore notified, ₹18,963.348 crore was accepted. Tamil Nadu raised ₹9,000 crore across four securities. Bihar SGS 2041 (re-issue) had the highest cut-off yield at 8.2811%. Assam accepted only ₹606.733 crore in its 9-year security and Nagaland ₹256.615 crore, while Gujarat, Jharkhand, Keralam (14-year) and Sikkim (SGS 2030) accepted nothing.
  • A fresh SGS auction of ₹29,900 crore is scheduled for November 03, 2026 (Tuesday). Twelve States are involved, and bids go on e-Kuber, with competitive bids from 10:30 to 11:30 A.M. and non-competitive bids from 10:30 to 11:00 A.M. Up to 10% of the notified amount is reserved for non-competitive bidders, capped at 1% of the notified amount per bid per stock. Payment is on November 04, and interest on new stock is paid half-yearly on May 04 and November 04. These securities count towards the Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
  • A 4-day Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction was held on October 26, 2026 for ₹1,50,000 crore. Bids of ₹1,86,666 crore were received and ₹1,50,007 crore was allotted. The cut-off rate was 5.55%, the weighted average rate 5.59%, and partial allotment at the cut-off rate was 85.07%. It is a liquidity-management operation under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility.
  • India's foreign exchange reserves stood at US$719,748 million (₹6,833,505 crore) on October 23, 2026. They fell US$1,269 million over the week. Foreign Currency Assets were US$593,205 million, gold US$103,150 million, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) US$18,605 million and the Reserve Position in the International Monetary Fund (IMF) US$4,787 million. The Weekly Statistical Supplement also shows reserves up US$28,640 million since end-March 2026.
  • RBI also released regular external-sector data. These were data on External Commercial Borrowings (ECB), Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB) and Rupee Denominated Bonds (RDB) for August 2026 (October 29), and Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) for September 2026 under the Automatic and Approval Routes (October 26). They matter for tracking capital-account flows.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • Lending and deposit rates data for October 2026 showed easing in fresh lending rates. The weighted average lending rate (WALR) on outstanding rupee loans of scheduled commercial banks (SCBs) stayed at 8.96% in September 2026. The WALR on fresh rupee loans fell to 8.51% from 8.61%, and the 1-year median Marginal Cost of Funds based Lending Rate (MCLR) fell to 8.55% in October 2026 from 8.61% in September. The weighted average domestic term deposit rate (WADTDR) was 6.55% on outstanding deposits (from 6.56%) and 5.61% on fresh deposits (from 5.67%).
  • Sectoral Deployment of Bank Credit for September 2026 showed sharply faster credit growth. Data came from 41 select SCBs covering about 95% of total non-food credit. Non-food bank credit grew 19.1% year-on-year (y-o-y), against 11.2% a year earlier. Agriculture and allied activities grew 19.5% (8.9% earlier), industry 19.5% (7.7%), services 28.3% (9.9%) and personal loans 17.3% (11.5%). Services growth was led by non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), trade, commercial real estate, transport operators and professional services, and growth in loans against gold jewellery and education slowed but stayed strong.
  • The Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025 changed how reporting fortnights are defined. From December 15, 2025 a fortnight means the 15th and last calendar day of a month, instead of alternate Fridays. For the sectoral credit return, the last reporting fortnight became the last day of the month from December 31, 2025. This affects how y-o-y growth rates are read, since comparisons use old-definition data for the previous year.
  • Weekly Statistical Supplement data for October 15, 2026 showed strong balance-sheet growth. Aggregate deposits were ₹27,875,910 crore (up 16.7% y-o-y) and bank credit ₹22,742,777 crore (up 18.2%). Broad money (M3) was ₹33,300,300 crore, up 16.0% y-o-y. Food credit outstanding for SCBs was ₹100,093 crore.
  • The Scheduled Banks' Statement of Position as on October 15, 2026 gave the banking-system count. It counted 120 scheduled commercial banks, 26 scheduled State co-operative banks and 55 scheduled primary (urban) co-operative banks. Bank credit (excluding inter-bank advances) was ₹22,742,777.30 crore and investments in Central and State government securities were ₹7,538,093.46 crore for SCBs.
  • ★ RBI extended Directions on three co-operative banks up to January 30, 2027. The banks are The Irinjalakuda Town Co-operative Bank Ltd., Kerala; Lokapavani Mahila Sahakari Bank Niyamitha, Mandya; and Sonpeth Nagari Sahakari Bank Maryadit, Sonpeth, Parbhani. The Directions were first issued under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, on July 29, 2025 for six months. They had already been extended to October 30, 2026 and now run three more months, subject to review. RBI stressed that an extension does not mean it is satisfied with the bank's financial position. This continues the pattern of repeated extensions seen with stressed urban co-operative banks (UCBs).
  • The Competition Commission of India (CCI) approved several acquisitions on October 31, 2026. The approvals involved IntelliSmart Infrastructure with Adani Energy Solutions, Navi Ltd with MIH Payments Holdings B.V., Sapphire Foods India with Devyani International, and Solenergi Power with Aditya Birla Renewables. CCI also approved the acquisition of 16.2% of the issued share capital of Vodafone Group PLC by Vega SAS, a holding company that is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Maya SAS.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

  • ★ The Cabinet approved the Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme with an outlay of ₹1,856.67 crore. It runs from FY 2026-27 to FY 2033-34 and aims to build a globally competitive sports-goods ecosystem. Anchor and turnover-linked support get ₹1,130 crore, certification support ₹105 crore and the remaining ₹621 crore goes to centres of excellence, start-ups, skilling and export promotion. It plans up to four Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones (SGMZs), each over 300 acres, converging with the BHAVYA scheme and NICDC Industrial Parks.
  • The Khelo India scheme gives specific incentive rates and market targets. Anchor investment support is 20% capital-expenditure aid for investors committing ₹100 crore or more. Turnover-linked support is up to 15% for manufacturers investing ₹10 crore or more. Certification support covers 70% of first-time and 50% of renewal costs for International Sports Federation certifications. The global sports equipment market is US$140 billion (2024), projected at US$283 billion by 2036, and India's equipment exports of US$275 million are targeted to reach US$8.1 billion by 2036, with 5.4 lakh potential jobs. It aligns with Khelo Bharat Niti 2025, Make in India and Viksit Bharat @ 2047.
  • ★ The Cabinet approved the ₹10,000 crore Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme (CMPS). It runs up to FY 2039-40 and is split as Interest Subvention Assistance ₹859 crore (3% per annum on term loans), Operational Expenditure Assistance ₹8,891 crore, testing and certification assistance ₹150 crore and scheme management ₹100 crore. It aims to raise annual domestic container capacity about tenfold, from 70,000 to 7.5 lakh Twenty-foot Equivalent Units (TEUs). It is expected to create about 3,000 direct and over 50,000 indirect jobs, and the per-container subsidy is ₹49,500 for 20-ft and ₹86,625 for 40-ft containers.
  • ★ The Cabinet approved the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM VGSY) with an outlay of ₹7,395.55 crore. This Centrally Sponsored Scheme covers 2026-27 to 2030-31, co-terminus with the 16th Finance Commission cycle. The Central share is ₹4,869.48 crore and the State share ₹2,526.07 crore. It targets about 32 lakh elected representatives and functionaries across about 2.55 lakh Panchayats. It aims at 2.55 lakh Gram Panchayat Development Plans and online audit plans each year, using eGramSwaraj and AuditOnline, with awareness episodes reaching at least 1 crore citizens each.
  • The Cabinet raised Dearness Allowance (DA) and Dearness Relief (DR) by 3% over the existing 60% rate. The increase is effective July 1, 2026 and follows the 7th Central Pay Commission recommendations. It costs the exchequer ₹10,016.10 crore per annum and benefits 48.54 lakh Central Government employees and 68.71 lakh pensioners.
  • ★ Union Budget 2026-27 set aside ₹250 crore for AVGC Content Creator Labs. The labs are for 15,000 secondary schools and 500 colleges, where AVGC stands for Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming and Comics, with XR (Extended Reality) usually added. The Indian Institute of Creative Technologies (IICT) is a National Centre of Excellence established in 2025, and the AVGC Promotion Task Force was constituted in April 2022. The GENESIS scheme was launched in 2022, and the market is projected to grow from USD 1,259.5 million (2023) to USD 7,818.7 million by 2030 at a 29.8% compound annual growth rate (CAGR).
  • ★ ₹1,000 crore of margin-money subsidy was released under the Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP). It supports 11,333 new micro-enterprises, with bank-sanctioned loans of about ₹3,260 crore and over 1.02 lakh jobs expected. In FY 2026-27 up to October 2026, 25,895 micro-enterprises received ₹794.52 crore of subsidy. Priority goes to women-led, SC/ST-owned, North-Eastern and Aspirational-District units.
  • ★ National Green Hydrogen Mission (NGHM) figures were restated in this week's material. The Mission was approved in January 2023 with an outlay of ₹19,744 crore and targets at least 5 million metric tonnes of green hydrogen annually by 2030. Its SIGHT programme has ₹17,490 crore up to 2029-30, and Strategic Hydrogen Innovation Partnership (SHIP) support is ₹400 crore. The Green Hydrogen Certification Scheme launched in April 2025, and ISTS charge exemption applies to plants commissioned by December 31, 2030.
  • The Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) marked its 74th Foundation Day and reported its progress. The wage ceiling for mandatory coverage rose from ₹15,000 (September 2014) to ₹25,000 (September 2026), and contributing members grew from 3.49 crore to about 7.98 crore. Reforms include consolidating 13 partial-withdrawal provisions into three categories, raising the auto-settlement limit to ₹5 lakh, EPFO services on WhatsApp and automatic settlement of eligible inoperative accounts with balances up to ₹1,000. Bhavishya Nidhi Awards 2026 were also announced.
  • The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) revised the MRP of 543 anti-cancer medicines. The new prices are effective November 9, 2026. Examples include Anastrozole 1 mg (10 tablets) falling from ₹695.76 to ₹73.00 and Bevacizumab 100 mg/4 ml from ₹21,767.70 to ₹5,930.13.
  • The National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) application deadline was extended to November 15, 2026. The scholarship is ₹12,000 per annum for Classes X to XII for fresh Class IX applicants, with 1 lakh fresh scholarships yearly. Parental income must not exceed ₹3.50 lakh a year, with a 5% relaxation for SC/ST. Verification dates are November 30, 2026 (Institute Nodal Officer) and December 15, 2026 (District Nodal Officer).
  • The 54th PRAGATI meeting reviewed infrastructure worth over ₹16,000 crore across 31 States/UTs. PRAGATI is the Prime Minister's ICT-enabled platform for proactive governance. The review covered Railways, Ekta Malls and Bulk Drug Parks in Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat and Himachal Pradesh, and urged major railway stations to be redeveloped as multimodal hubs.
  • Other schemes and data points in the week's releases: The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment's Shilp Samagam Mela 2026 runs from October 31 to November 15 at Dilli Haat, INA. The Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maandhan (PM-SYM) pension for unorganised workers is ₹3,000 per month after age 60. Customs duty exemption on cotton was extended to December 31, 2026 (Notification No. 35/2026-Customs, October 30, 2026).

Economy & Trade

  • The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) Flash Report gave a snapshot of central infrastructure projects. As of September 2026 there were 1,853 ongoing central sector projects with a total latest cost of ₹37.56 lakh crore. Cumulative expenditure was ₹17.73 lakh crore (about 47.21% of cost), and there were 780 mega projects (₹1,000 crore or more) and 1,073 major projects. Transport and Logistics has 1,320 projects (71.2% of projects) costing ₹20.07 lakh crore, and Energy has 225 projects (12.1%) costing ₹11.04 lakh crore.
  • The Flash Report also listed commissioned projects and new additions. Buxar Thermal Power (1320 MW, ₹13,756.56 crore) was commissioned on 12-09-2026 and inaugurated on 14-09-2026. The Delhi-Amritsar-Katra Expressway Phase-I Package-I (₹2,429.90 crore) and the NMDC Slurry Pipeline Phase-1 (₹5,427 crore) were also listed. Newly on-boarded projects through the PAIMANA portal include the Krishnagiri Renewable Energy Zone Phase-I transmission (₹7,627 crore) and the Hosur-Omalur Umbrella work (₹1,839.32 crore).
  • India's services trade stayed strong in September 2026. Services exports were US$39,305 million (up 7.2% y-o-y) and imports US$20,097 million (up 12.6%). For July they were US$38,253 million and US$20,606 million, and for August US$35,471 million and US$18,934 million. July to September data are provisional and growth rates are revised on the basis of balance of payments statistics.
  • India's installed power capacity reached 558.9 GW on September 30, 2026. Of this, 308 GW (55%) is non-fossil. The Ministry of Power reported 53 lakh rooftop solar systems under PM-Surya Ghar and PM-KUSUM figures of 12 lakh standalone solar pumps and 17 lakh solarised grid-connected pumps (16.7 GW). These came from the Minister of State's visit to Singapore International Energy Week (October 26-28).
  • The Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo (BRESE) 2026 runs from November 2 to 5, 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. It expects over 130 countries and 13 Indian States/UTs, and is co-located with the 9th International Solar Alliance (ISA) Assembly and the 4th International Conference on Green Hydrogen. The theme is 'Invest. Innovate. Inspire.', with an expected USD 220 billion of investment during 2027-30.
  • GeM Samvād 2026 announced reforms for MSME public procurement. The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) cut its transaction charge from 0.50% to 0.30% of order value and raised the exemption threshold to orders up to ₹10 lakh from ₹5 lakh. For orders above ₹10 crore the charge is capped at ₹3 lakh instead of ₹72.5 lakh. GeM integrated all five Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS) exchanges and promoted GeM SAHAY, which offers collateral-free lending against purchase orders.
  • The Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises (ASUSE) 2027-28 will run from April 2027 to March 2028. New question areas include gender-specific constraints faced by women entrepreneurs, fixed-asset investment over a longer reference period, electricity sources and identification of handloom, power-loom and handicraft units. States can generate district-level estimates, and feedback is due by November 15, 2026.
  • Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) data showed record sector performance for FY 2025-26. Turnover was ₹1.87 lakh crore and employment over 2.04 crore people, with a target of ₹2.51 lakh crore for the next fiscal. The Khadi Diwali Fest-2026 offers 20% off on Khadi and 10% off on Village Industry products at NDMC Palika Park, Connaught Place.
  • The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) seized e-cigarettes and live reptiles. At Syama Prasad Mookerjee Port, Kolkata, about 55,000 e-cigarettes worth over ₹13.50 crore were seized (October 24, 2026), mis-declared as 'water glass' and 'ceramic mugs' and bound for Nepal. DRI seizures in FY 2026-27 total about 3,00,000 e-cigarettes worth over ₹120 crore. At Trichy airport, 45 live reptiles were recovered and two passengers were arrested under the Customs Act, 1962.

International Affairs & Organizations

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla inaugurated it at Samvidhan Sadan under the theme 'Fostering Parliaments for People-Centric Development'. Radhakrishnan said so while meeting a delegation of the European Parliament's Committee on Foreign Affairs (AFET) led by David McAllister.   • The Indian Navy's INS Sahyadri reached Busan, South Korea on October 26, 2026 for a bilateral exercise. The harbour phase has cross-deck visits, cross-training and sporting events, and the sea phase has coordinated drills with ROKS Munmu the Great.   • The first BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise runs from October 30 to November 3, 2026. The participants are Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Thailand, and the aims are interoperability and maritime domain awareness. BIMSTEC is the Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation.
  • Prime Minister Narendra Modi congratulated Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva on his Brazil Presidential victory on October 26, 2026. He pledged to deepen the India-Brazil Strategic Partnership. For exam purposes, Brazil is among countries with which India has not signed a Free Trade Agreement.
  • 108 stupas were raised at Gelephu Mindfulness City, Bhutan on November 1, 2026. Each stupa is 15 metres tall and they are spaced 108 metres apart along an 11.7 km stretch. Union Minister Kiren Rijiju attended with King Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck and Prime Minister Dasho Tshering Tobgay. The King dedicated Stupa No. 76 to PM Modi.
  • India released four biodiversity publications at the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) COP-17 in Yerevan. They were the Guidelines for Designation of India's Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures (I-OECMs) 2026, the 5th National Report on the Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety, the Convention on Biodiversity Handbook and India's Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) Success Story.   • India-Bhutan hydropower cooperation featured in energy diplomacy at Singapore International Energy Week. Bilateral talks with Bhutan covered Punatsangchhu-I (1200 MW) and Punatsangchhu-II (1020 MW), and the talks with Singapore covered cross-border interconnection. India also engaged the International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA) and the International Energy Agency (IEA).

Sports / Environment / Science & Tech

  • Cyclone ARNAB was reviewed by the National Crisis Management Committee (NCMC) under the Cabinet Secretary on October 26, 2026. At 0530 hrs IST on October 26 it lay 590 km north-west of Sri Vijaya Puram (Andaman) and 650 km east-south-east of Vishakhapatnam. It was expected to move west-north-westward till October 27 and then north-westward towards the Andhra Pradesh coast, with maximum sustained winds of 115-125 kmph and gusts to 140 kmph on the forenoon of October 28. Impact zones included Andhra Pradesh, Yanam, Telangana, Southern Odisha, Central Chhattisgarh, Eastern Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, and fishermen were advised to stay ashore from October 26 to 29.
  • A 19-month-old male cheetah (KNG22) died at Kuno National Park on October 27, 2026. Five firearm pellets had been recovered during emergency surgery on October 25. A high-level Central team of the Inspector General of Forests (National Tiger Conservation Authority) and the Additional Director and Regional Deputy Director of the Wildlife Crime Control Bureau will investigate with the Madhya Pradesh Forest Department. It matters for questions on Project Cheetah and wildlife protection.
  • The Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave (ESTIC) 2026 was held from October 27 to 29 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. The Prime Minister inaugurated it on October 27, with over 5,000 delegates and 12 priority thematic areas, including AI, biotechnology, advanced materials, manufacturing, healthcare, energy, climate and ocean sciences. The Prime Minister said India's startup ecosystem grew from about 400 (2014) to over 2.3 lakh (2026), with 50-60% in Tier-2/3 cities. Exhibits included CSIR-Designer Rice (20% protein, glycaemic index 54).
  • ISRO's NVS-03 navigation satellite was launched on GSLV-F18. The Prime Minister praised the launch on October 31, 2026, and it supports the Navigation with Indian Constellation (NavIC) regional system and self-reliance in navigation.
