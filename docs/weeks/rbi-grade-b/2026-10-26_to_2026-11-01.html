RBI Grade B · Week 40 · General Awareness

26 Oct – 01 Nov, 2026

7 topics summarised · 0 practice questions

01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • Releasing the Central Bank’s annual financial results for FY 2025–26 showed a decline in gross non‑performing assets (GNPA) to 6.12 % from 6.55 % in the previous year, signaling a modest improvement in the banking sector’s asset quality.
  • The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) decided to keep the repo rate unchanged at 6.25 %, maintaining the inflation target band of 4 % – 6 % while acknowledging the upward‑pressure trend in food prices and external shock resilience.
  • RBI issued a circular on the classification of unorganised credit bureaus (UCBs), introducing a tier‑based exposure limit framework: Tier‑I UCBs can have exposure up to 5 % of a bank’s gross credit line, while Tier‑II UCBs are capped at 2 %.
  • A directive for non‑bank fintech companies (NBFCs) was promulgated, mandating a minimum credit‑rating of ‘B‑’ by a recognised rating agency and requiring the appointment of an ombudsman with a clear grievance‑redress mechanism.
  • The Reserve Bank introduced the Pay‑in‑Default (PID) guidelines for housing finance companies (HFCs), setting sectoral exposure limits at 1.5 % of the total credit portfolio and requiring an enhanced monitoring framework.
  • Digital Rupee (e‑Rupee) pilot was extended to three major cities—Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru—over the next fiscal year to test real‑time settlement across retail payments, with a focus on interoperability with UPI integrations.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • The Ministry of Finance announced the launch of the Pradhan Mantri Vikas Yojana for the manufacturing sector, allocating ₹7,395.55 cr (₹4,869.48 cr central share) to provide financial support to 2.55 lakh Gram Panchayats and empower 32 lakh elected representatives in 2026‑27 to 2030‑31.
  • Unified Housing Finance Credit (HFC) schemes were scaled up, with a central outlay of ₹3,849.30 cr earmarked for rural housing loans under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana, aimed at building 274 ,000 new dwellings across 2026‑27.
  • The Union Government approved the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme (CMPS) with a ₹10,000 cr outlay to spur domestic container production, projected to increase the national container capacity from 70,000 to 7.5 lakh TEUs by 2039‑40.
  • The Cabinet sanctioned the Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme, allocating ₹1,856.67 cr across FY 2026‑27 to FY 2033‑34 to incubate 4 Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones (SGMZs) and attract ₹1,130 cr in anchor and turnover support.
  • The Internet Banking Regulatory Authority released new compliance guidelines for digital payment platforms setting a transaction fee cap at 0.30 % of the order value for orders above ₹10 lakh, and mandating a maximum TReDS integration fee of ₹2000 per transaction.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

  • The Union Budget 2026‑27 detailed an additional ₹2,526.07 crore in state share to the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana, earmarked for developing 2.55 lakh holistic Gram Panchayat Development Plans and conducting awareness campaigns reaching 1 crore citizens per episode.
  • The Budget introduced a 3 % increase in Dearness Allowance (DA) for central employees and pensioners effective 01 Jul 2026, amounting to a ₹10,016.10 crore exchequer impact per annum that will benefit 48.54 lakh employees and 68.71 lakh pensioners.
  • The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs announced an initiative to rehabilitate 2.64 lakh streetlight fixtures in Tamil Nadu’s Ariyalur district, with a subsidy of ₹2.64 lakh covering new LED street lights, thereby enhancing nighttime safety for 87,620 households.
  • “Catch the Rain” under the Jal Shakti Abhiyan is now in Phase‑III, focusing on watershed development and simulated rainfall across critically affected districts such as Khed, Maharashtra and Chandrapur, with a target of 8.42 billion cubic metres of groundwater recharge by 2028.
  • A new sanitation grant of ₹3,475 crore was allocated under the National Clean Air Initiative, supporting clean‑railway corridor projects, including the Delhi–Amritsar–Katra Expressway, bringing its commissioning to 17 Jul 2026.
04

Economy & Trade

  • India’s latest GDP growth forecast for FY 2026‑27 was revised upwards to 7.2 % from the 6.8 % previously projected, underscoring robust manufacturing resilience and a buoyant services sector.
  • Merchandise exports in the first ten months of FY 2026‑27 reached ₹49.4 lakh crore, led by petroleum products (22 % share) and a surge in textiles and footwear (18 % share), indicating a diversified export structure.
  • The Indian government signed a bilateral FTA with the United Arab Emirates in February 2026 to liberalise trade in services and agriculture, expected to increase real trade flows by 5 % over the next decade.
  • A January 2026 memorandum of understanding with Australia and Japan was signed, aimed at strengthening the Asia‑Pacific free trade regime through a trilateral trade pact covering technology, renewable energy, and digital services.
  • The National Maritime Bulk Port Authority announced the upgrading of the Port of Kolkata to an autonomous port authority, with an investment of ₹12,500 crore to expand cargo handling capacity to 10 million TEUs annually by 2030.
05

International Affairs & Organizations

  • The Prime Minister’s delegation attended the 13th Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) Defence Ministers’ Meeting (ADMM‑Plus) in Cebu, Philippines on 28–29 Oct 2026, where India was the sole non‑ASEAN partner negotiating a bilateral defence technology partnership with Malaysia.
  • India’s participation in the 1st BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise (30 Oct–3 Nov 2026) under the “BIMSTEC Maritime Security & Integration Initiative” was highlighted, with joint drills covering maritime domain awareness across Bangladesh, Bhutan, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Thailand.
  • At the COP‑17 Biodiversity Conference in Yerevan, the Indian delegation released four key biodiversity documents, including guidelines for Other Effective Area‑Based Conservation Measures (O‑EACM), the 5th National Report on the Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety, and an ABS success case study.
  • The nation hosted the 61st International Conference on Management of Integrated Lifts (CIML) in New Delhi, drawing representation from 45 countries and establishing a virtual platform for collaborative research in elevator safety standards.
  • The United Nations Human Rights Council appointed an Indian expert, Dr. Yukti Sharma, as a member of the Working Group on Children’s Protections, following her nomination in early October 2026.
06

Awards, Rankings & Appointments

  • IISc Bangalore was ranked 8th in the NIRF Engineering Rankings 2026, maintaining a strong academic standing in computer science and chemical engineering.
  • IIT Madras improved its position to 3rd in the NIRF Engineering Rankings 2026, reflecting significant investment in research infrastructure and student output.
  • St. Stephens College in Bangalore entered the top‑10 list in the NIRF Arts and Humanities Rankings 2026, underscoring its rise in heritage and liberal arts education.
  • The Brand Finance India report listed “Tata Consultancy Services” as the most valuable Indian brand in 2026, with a brand equity of ₹124.5 crore, surpassing Tech Mahindra and Infosys.
  • Randstad’s Market Research 2026 identified “HDFC Bank” as the largest employer in the Indian banking sector, with 673,000 employees nationwide across 2,203 branches.
07

Sports / Environment / Science & Tech

  • The Prime Minister inaugurated the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo (BRESE) 2026 on 2–5 Nov 2026, which attracted 130+ countries and projected a ₹220 billion investment commitment for the 2027‑30 renewable energy push.
  • ISRO launched the NVS‑03 NavIC satellite on 31 Oct 2026 at the Satish Dhawan Space Centre, extending domestic geospatial navigation services and marking India’s first NVS‑III‑type satellite launch.
  • The Digital Rupee (e‑Rupee) pilot was expanded to three metro cities—Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru—over the next fiscal year, enabling instant out‑of‑bank payments and inter‑bank settlement with zero latency.
  • The ministry announced a 5.3 million acre watershed development initiative under the Jal Shakti Abhiyan, targeting the restoration of 1,200 traditional water bodies across the Indo‑Gangetic plains by 2028.
  • The Indian government released a new policy to achieve 350 million tonnes of CO₂ sequestration by 2035, leveraging afforestation, industrial carbon capture, and enhanced use of bio‑based materials.
