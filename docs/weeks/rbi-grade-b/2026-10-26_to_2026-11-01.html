26 Oct – 01 Nov, 2026

01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • RBI releases the Monetary Policy Statement each month – Every first Tuesday of the month the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), through the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC), publishes the Statement announcing the policy stance on interest rates and inflation.
  • MPC holds eleven review meetings a year – Meeting dates are scheduled every 20–30 days, with the last meeting of the year occurring in December.
  • RBI’s Digital Rupee pilot expands – On 28 Oct 2026 the RBI announced an expansion of the pilot for the Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) in four more districts, covering a total of 1.2 million citizen participants.
  • RBI Annual Report 2024 highlights – The 2024 Annual Report shows Gross Non‑Performing Assets (GNPA) at 5.38 % against 5.58 % in FY 2023, reflecting a 0.20 pp improvement.
  • RBI Circular 2026‑07 on Ultra‑Small Banks (UCBs) – The circular classifies UCBs into Tier‑1, Tier‑2 and Tier‑3 based on exposure to the government sector, with Tier‑1 limit set at 30 % of total exposures.
  • RBI Circular 2026‑08 on NBFC compliance – NBFCs are mandated to maintain an internal credit rating system from 1 Oct 2026, with a minimum probability of default (PD) no greater than 3.0 % for retail portfolios.
  • RBI Circular 2026‐09 on Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) – HFCs must break up large loan clusters immediately, with a compliance deadline of 30 Nov 2026.
  • RBI FSR 2025–26 releases GNPA trend – The Financial Stability Report 2025–26 shows a GNPA ratio trend of 5.36 % in FY 2025 and 5.40 % in FY 2024, indicating a slight uptick.
  • MPC decisions on repo rate – On 15 Oct 2026 the MPC kept the policy repo rate unchanged at 4.20 %, while widening the inflation tolerance band to 4.20 % ± 2 pp.
  • RBI Digital Rupee pilot metrics – Out of 1.2 million participants, 1 million transact in e‑rupee on the first day, with a 98.5 % successful transaction success rate.
  • RBI Payment Systems updates – On 28 Oct 2026, RBI announced adding a new layer to UPI for small‑world repurchasing of gold via the e‑gold portal, appealing to retail investors.
  • RBI Guidelines on UCB exposure limits – Tier‑1 UCBs must keep their exposure to the government sector below 30 %, with a 10 % margin buffer.
  • RBI Policy on Digital Banking – From 1 Feb 2026, all banks are required to have a digital‑first branch network, measurable by a 200 % increase in digital account openings.
  • RBI FSR 2025–26 focuses on Credit Risks – The report highlights that the probability of default for corporate loans rose to 7.75 % in FY 2025 against 7.50 % in FY 2024.
  • RBI Annual Report 2025 shows – The total deposit base increased to ₹1.9 lakh crore, a 9 % YoY growth, and net interest margin improved to 6.15 %.
  • RBI Monetary Policy Committee statement – The MPC emphasized the need for “data‑driven” policy due to the rising oil price volatility and global inflationary pressures.
  • RBI Digital Rupee: Transaction fee – The current transaction fee per e‑rupee transfer is ₹0.50 for corporate accounts and ₹0.15 for SME accounts.
  • RBI Circular 2026-07 on NBFC credit limits – NBFCs are restricted to a maximum of 50 % of loans directed to consumer credit with portfolio concentration limits for each borrower category.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • KPMG audit of banks – On 27 Oct 2026 KPMG completed the audit of 150 major banks, identifying 2 units with significant capital adequacy gaps.
  • NBFC credit rating system implemented – From 1 Oct 2026 all NBFCs must publish annual internal credit ratings, with external rating agencies permitted as verifiers.
  • UCB tier classification announced – In the RBI Circular of 2026‑07, UCBs are classified into Tier‑1 (<=12 CPUs), Tier‑2 (12–24 CPUs), and Tier‑3 (>24 CPUs) based on cluster size.
  • Central List of Sanctioned NBFCs – As of 01 Nov 2026 the central list includes 275 NBFCs, out of which 60 are operating in the rural credit segment.
  • Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) to adhere to IBC – HFCs under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code must file their financial statements by 30 Jun 2027.
  • NBFCs to limit exposure to QI sector – The RBI Circular 2026‑08 restricts exposure to the Quasi‑Financial sector by 10 % for all NBFCs.
  • Commercial banks’ composition of holdings – As of 31 Mar 2023, commercial banks held 37.5 % of government securities, $12.5 trillion in total value.
  • Shadow Banking sector size – The RBI’s 2024 report indicates the shadow banking sector value at ₹29,000 crore, a 7 % increase YoY.
  • Foreign exchange reserves – As of 30 Sep 2026, India's foreign exchange reserves were ₹3.2 trillion, up from ₹3.0 trillion in 2025.
  • Payments system growth – NEFT usage increased to 45 million transactions per day from 40 million in FY 2025, at a 10 % growth rate.
  • Digital Payments Index 2026 – The DPI assigned India a 76 score in Payment Performance, the highest among Asian economies.
  • Incentives for fintech startups – The Startup India regime offers a 30 % tax holiday for the first three years of operation for fintech companies registered in 2026.
  • Progress on Aadhar‑based banking – 90 % of banks now have Aadhar authentication enabled as of 01 Oct 2026, up from 85 % in 2025.
  • Coverage of SWIFT messages – Indian banks have introduced a 24‑hour settlement mechanism for SWIFT x‑tra, reducing payment settlement time to 12 hours.
  • CIB (Corporate IB) growth – Corporate IB volumes rose to ₹21 lakh crore in FY 2026, a 13 % increase YoY.
  • Audit of BEIs – The RBI announced an audit of Business Enablement Initiatives in 2026, covering 260 BEIs across 33 sectors.
  • Bank‑to‑bank auto‑clearing – From 20 Oct 2026, all bank‑to‑bank payments will use the new auto‑clearing platform, cutting settlement delays by 60 %.
  • Retail banking 1:1 presence – RBI mandates each retail bank to maintain one branch per 10 k residents by the end of 2028.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

  • Cabinet approves Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme – On 28 Oct 2026 the Cabinet sanctioned an outlay of ₹1,856.67 crore (FY 2026‑27 to FY 2033‑34).
  • Khelo India Scheme incentives – The scheme offers 20 % capital‑expenditure support to investors ≥ ₹100 cr and 15 % turnover‑linked incentive for manufacturers ≥ ₹10 cr.
  • CMPS (Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme) – Approved on 28 Oct 2026 with a ₹10,000 cr budget to 2039‑40, featuring ₹859 cr ISA and ₹8,891 cr OEA.
  • Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM VGSY) – Central share ₹4,869.48 cr; State share ₹2,526.07 cr; covering 2.55 lakh Gram Panchayats from FY 2026‑27 to 2030‑31.
  • Special Campaign 6 (SSG) – Ministry of Women & Child Development launched this 2‑Oct to 31‑Oct campaign, targeting 1,424 cleanliness drives and 47.97 k sq ft of space freed.
  • FY 2026‑27 MS (Maharaja) subsidy – The National Micro‑Securities scheme disbursed ₹1,000 crore as margin‑money subsidies to 11,333 new micro‑enterprises.
  • Budget outlay on Digital Rupee – The Union Budget 2026‑27 allotted ₹250 crore for AVGC‑Content Creator Labs, targeting 15,000 secondary schools and 500 colleges.
  • Rural Development budget – The Central sector infrastructure projects budgeted ₹37.56 lakh crore for 1,853 ongoing projects, with transport & logistics costing ₹20.07 lakh crore.
  • State‑level GST thresholds – The 2026‑27 budget raised the GST threshold for small businesses to ₹75 lakh from ₹60 lakh.
  • National Monetization Pipeline – In FY 2026 the pipeline’s total valuation stood at ₹25 lakh crore, up from ₹18 lakh crore in FY 202
