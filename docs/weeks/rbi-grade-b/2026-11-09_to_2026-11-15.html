01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • ★ No Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) decision was announced in this week's releases; the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) focused on liquidity management and debt auctions. The MPC works under a flexible inflation-targeting framework with a 4% target and a 6% upper tolerance limit, and the target is treated as missed if inflation stays above 6% for three consecutive quarters. Revise the current repo rate from the latest policy statement. The auction rates quoted this week (about 5.47%–5.54%) are market outcomes in liquidity operations, not the policy rate.
  • RBI conducted a 4-day Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction on 09 Nov 2026 to inject liquidity. The notified amount was ₹50,000 crore, bids received were ₹62,872 crore and the amount allotted was ₹50,003 crore. The cut-off rate was 5.51%, the weighted average rate was 5.54% and partial allotment at the cut-off was 15.18%. VRR is a Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) tool used when the system is short of funds.
  • Two Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auctions were held on 12 and 13 Nov 2026 to absorb surplus liquidity. The overnight VRRR (12 Nov) had a notified amount of ₹50,000 crore, offers of ₹58,149 crore and acceptances of ₹50,012 crore. Its cut-off rate was 5.49%, weighted average rate 5.47% and partial acceptance 59.56%. The 3-day VRRR (13 Nov, window 09:30–10:00 AM, reversal 16 Nov) had a notified amount of ₹75,000 crore, and offers of ₹38,592 crore were all accepted at a cut-off and weighted average rate of 5.49%. Operational guidelines remain those of Press Release 2019-2020/1947 dated 13 Feb 2020.
  • The Weekly Statistical Supplement (WSS) shows heavy net liquidity absorption through the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF). For 02–08 Nov 2026 the daily net absorption ranged from ₹1,46,453 crore (07 Nov) to ₹2,13,730 crore (06 Nov), and SDF balances were ₹2,02,470 crore on 06 Nov. The SDF is the floor of the LAF corridor, and the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) is the ceiling. Loans and advances to State Governments stood at ₹15,415 crore on 06 Nov 2026, down ₹21,522 crore over the year.
  • ★ India's total foreign exchange reserves were ₹67,58,986 crore (US$719,547 million) as on 06 Nov 2026. Foreign Currency Assets were US$591,350 million, Gold US$104,796 million, Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) US$18,610 million and the Reserve Position in the International Monetary Fund (IMF) US$4,791 million. In the week the reserves fell ₹49,742 crore in rupee terms but rose US$2,490 million in dollar terms. They were up US$28,439 million from end-March 2026 and US$32,513 million year-on-year.
  • ★ The Government of India (GoI) re-issued 6.94% GS 2036 (maturing 11 May 2036) for ₹34,000 crore, with the auction on 13 Nov 2026 and settlement on 16 Nov 2026. The cut-off price was ₹97.43 (yield to maturity 7.3203%) and the weighted average price ₹97.46 (yield 7.3158%). RBI received 349 competitive bids worth ₹73,016.600 crore and accepted 146 worth ₹33,947.718 crore, with 18.3591% partial allotment (34 bids). Eight non-competitive bids of ₹52.282 crore were accepted in full, and devolvement on Primary Dealers (PDs) was nil. GoI held the option to retain up to ₹2,000 crore extra, and "When Issued" trading ran from 11 to 13 Nov.
  • The underwriting auction for 6.94% GS 2036 fixed the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) commission cut-off at 0.54 paise per ₹100. The Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) was ₹810 crore per PD (₹17,010 crore in total), with ₹16,990 crore accepted under ACU, so the full ₹34,000 crore was underwritten. ACU bids were taken on e-Kuber between 09:00 and 09:30 AM. This follows the underwriting scheme notified on 14 Nov 2007.
  • ★ All G-sec and State Government Securities (SGS) bids are placed on the RBI Core Banking Solution, e-Kuber. Competitive bids are accepted between 10:30 and 11:30 AM and non-competitive bids between 10:30 and 11:00 AM, in multiples of ₹10,000, and physical bids are accepted only if the system fails. Under the multiple-price method, successful bidders pay their own quoted price or yield. Non-resident investment follows the Fully Accessible Route, and individuals can bid through the Retail Direct portal. Together with INFINET and the Clearing Corporation of India Ltd (CCIL), e-Kuber is a core RBI market infrastructure topic.
  • SGS auction results (09 Nov 2026): States accepted ₹27,083.882 crore against ₹29,050 crore notified. Bihar accepted only ₹1,533.882 crore of its ₹2,000 crore 18-year security, and Chhattisgarh accepted nothing. Cut-offs included Assam 2046 at 8.0814%, Gujarat 10-year at 7.82%, Haryana 4-year at 7.61% and Keralam 2032 at 7.8109%, and Tamil Nadu raised ₹9,000 crore across three October re-issues plus one older security. This was a large, mostly re-issue-based borrowing cycle with yields mostly around 8%.
  • RBI announced a further SGS auction for ₹23,300 crore to be held on 17 Nov 2026, with 10 States participating. The States are Andhra Pradesh, Goa, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan, Telangana, Tripura and West Bengal. Up to 10% of each stock's notified amount goes to non-competitive bidders, with a 1% cap per single bid, and new stock pays interest half-yearly on 18 May and 18 Nov. SGS are eligible for Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) investment under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, and are governed by the Government Securities Act, 2006.
  • Treasury Bill (T-bill) auction result of 11 Nov 2026 and the next auction for 18 Nov 2026. Cut-off yields were 5.8681% (91-day, price ₹98.5581), 6.5183% (182-day, ₹96.8521) and 6.6394% (364-day, ₹93.7900), against notified amounts of ₹8,000 crore, ₹8,000 crore and ₹7,000 crore. The next auction totals ₹23,000 crore with settlement on 19 Nov 2026, uses the multiple-price method, and caps retail allocation at 5% of the notified amount.
  • GoI announced a conversion/switch auction of ₹25,000 crore on 16 Nov 2026, with settlement on T+1. Short-dated source securities (for example 7.38% GS 2027 and 7.04% GS 2029) are swapped for longer destination securities such as 7.18% GS 2037 and 7.40% GS 2062. The source price must equal the Financial Benchmarks India Pvt. Ltd. (FBIL) closing price of the previous working day, and the switch ratio is rounded to 8 decimal places. The minimum bid is ₹10,000, and the exercise lengthens debt maturity without new cash borrowing.
  • ★ The Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) 2018-19 Series-III (issued 13 Nov 2018) reached final redemption on 13 Nov 2026 at ₹14,024 per unit. SGBs are repayable after eight years, and the price is the simple average of the closing price of 999-purity gold of the previous three business days (10, 11 and 12 Nov 2026) published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA). The tranche was issued under GoI notification F.No.4(22)-B(W&M)/2018 dated 08 Oct 2018.
  • ★ RBI imposed a ₹9.00 lakh penalty on One Mobikwik Systems Limited by an order dated 06 Nov 2026 for breaching Prepaid Payment Instrument (PPI) and fraud-reporting directions. The charges were allowing PPI holders to transfer funds to non-pre-registered beneficiaries beyond the prescribed limit and failing to report certain frauds on time. The penalty is under Section 30(1) read with Section 26(6) of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, following an inspection covering June 2025 to March 2026. RBI clarified the action is about regulatory compliance and does not judge the validity of any customer transaction.
  • RBI also released data on External Commercial Borrowings (ECB), Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB) and Rupee Denominated Bonds (RDB) for September 2026 on 12 Nov, and Reserve Money and Money Supply data for the fortnight ended 31 Oct 2026 on 11 Nov. These are routine monthly and fortnightly statistical releases. The money supply numbers are in the Banking section.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • ★ RBI extended the supersession of the Board of Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank Ltd., Mumbai, by one more year from 24 Nov 2026. The Board was first superseded for one year on 24 Nov 2023 over poor governance, and was extended from 24 Nov 2024 and 24 Nov 2025. The power used is Section 36AAA read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. Satya Prakash Pathak continues as Administrator, assisted by a Committee of Advisors (Venkatesh Hegde, Devendra Kumar and Suhas Gokhale), which is a typical Urban Co-operative Bank (UCB) supervisory action.
  • ★ RBI cancelled the Certificate of Registration (CoR) of Gandhinagar Leasing and Finance Ltd., a Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) based in Ahmedabad, effective 29 Oct 2026. The CoR No. was 01.00279, dated 08 Dec 1998, and the power used is Section 45-IA(6) of the RBI Act, 1934. The company can no longer carry on the business of a non-banking financial institution as defined in Section 45-I(a). CoR cancellations are routine NBFC regulatory actions that exams often test.
  • Aggregate deposits of Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs) were ₹2,82,51,076 crore on 31 Oct 2026 (about ₹28,251,076 crore), up 16.9% year-on-year. In the same Weekly Statistical Supplement, bank credit was ₹2,31,82,499 crore, up 19.5% year-on-year. Demand deposits were ₹34,76,854 crore and time deposits ₹2,47,74,222 crore. Credit growth outpacing deposit growth is a theme to remember.
  • The Scheduled Banks' Statement of Position as on 31 Oct 2026 covered 120 Scheduled Commercial Banks, 26 Scheduled State Co-operative Banks and 55 Scheduled Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks. The SCB category includes Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Small Finance Banks (SFBs), Payments Banks (PBs) and Local Area Banks (LABs). Food credit of SCBs was ₹1,23,266.71 crore (against ₹70,371.31 crore a year earlier), and borrowings of SCBs from RBI were ₹29,134 crore. Statement-based questions often test the count of bank categories.
  • Broad money (M3) stood at ₹3,37,68,416 crore on 31 Oct 2026, up 16.5% year-on-year. Currency with the public was ₹42,67,896 crore (up 14.7%), time deposits ₹2,57,38,861 crore (up 17.4%) and demand deposits ₹36,34,178 crore (up 12.0%). Net bank credit to Government was ₹95,07,646 crore and bank credit to the commercial sector ₹2,41,13,521 crore (up 19.4%). Time deposits were the fastest-growing component.
  • Under the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025, the reporting "fortnight" changed from alternate Fridays to the 15th and the last calendar day of a month, effective 15 Dec 2025. The latest data are therefore reported as of 15 Oct and 31 Oct. Year-ago comparisons still use the old reporting Fridays until the press communique as on 30 Nov 2026. The change aligns reporting dates with calendar-month data.
  • Tamilnad Mercantile Bank (TMB) celebrated its 105th Foundation Day at Thoothukudi, addressed by Vice-President C. P. Radhakrishnan on 11 Nov 2026. TMB began in 1921 as Nadar Bank Limited and now has 632 branches across 17 States and 4 Union Territories, over 53 lakh customers and total business above ₹1.30 lakh crore. Its leadership includes Part-Time Chairman K. Ramachandran and MD & CEO Salee S. Nair. The Vice-President urged banks to support MSMEs, farmers and women-led enterprises and to strengthen cybersecurity.
  • The Fund of Funds for Startups (FFS), run by the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), shows how development finance feeds banking and capital markets. SIDBI has committed ₹12,623 crore to 166 Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), which invested ₹28,254 crore in 1,475 startups (Oct 2026). FFS 2.0, notified in April 2026, carries a separate ₹10,000 crore corpus for deep-tech and early-growth startups. The Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (CGSS) has guaranteed 532 loans worth ₹1,662.71 crore.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

  • ★ The Union Cabinet approved continuation of the Stand-Up India scheme for 5 years, up to 31.03.2031, with a Credit Guarantee Corpus of ₹4,111 crore. Launched in April 2016, it has facilitated loans to over 2.75 lakh Women, SC and ST entrepreneurs and now targets 2.5 lakh more. Greenfield loans above ₹10 lakh and up to ₹1 crore (up to 85% of project cost) carry 85% credit guarantee coverage and a standard 0.50% guarantee fee. Eligible sectors are manufacturing, services, trading and agri-allied activities.
  • ★ The Cabinet also continued the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU) up to 31.03.2031, with a ₹5,000 crore corpus. It is expected to support PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY) loans of ₹12.6 lakh crore over five years and is operated through the National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Limited (NCGTC). The guarantee fee falls from 1% to 0.75% p.a., while Self Help Groups (SHGs) stay at 0.25% for year one and 0.50% afterwards. It applies across the XVIth Finance Commission cycle (FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31).
  • ★ PMMY (introduced 2015) offers collateral-free loans up to ₹20 lakh, with a 60.36 crore+ loan, ₹43.34 lakh crore cumulative record as of September 2026. Categories are Shishu (up to ₹50,000), Kishor (₹50,000–₹5 lakh), Tarun (₹5–10 lakh) and Tarun Plus (₹10–20 lakh). As of 30 Oct 2026, 2.44 crore+ loans worth over ₹3.3 lakh crore were sanctioned in FY 2026-27. The CGFMU also covers overdraft up to ₹10,000 on Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) accounts.
  • ★ Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) rates for Rabi 2026-27 (01.10.2026 to 31.03.2027) carry a budgetary requirement of about ₹62,370.02 crore. This is ₹24,417.73 crore more than Rabi 2025-26 (₹37,952.29 crore). The scheme has been in force since 01.04.2010 and covers 28 grades of Phosphatic and Potassic (P&K) fertilizers, including DAP, with subsidy paid to manufacturers and importers. Rates track international prices of Urea, DAP, MOP and Sulphur.
  • ★ The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) approved the Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum (YYBM) at ₹3,230 crore. It will be developed by adaptive reuse of the North and South Block complex, New Delhi, with a built-up area of about 1,78,509 sq m, making it described as having the world's largest display area. The Public Investment Board appraised it on 27 July 2026 and the PM announced it on 18 May 2023. Implementation runs FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31, with the first gallery (Adornment) due in North Block in late 2027.
  • The Cabinet approved extension of the Aqua Line Metro from Noida Sector-51 to Greater Noida Sector-04. The stretch is 8.449 km with 5 elevated stations and interchange with the Blue Line at Sector-61. The total active network of Noida and Greater Noida becomes 71.878 km. This is part of the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) urban transport push.
  • National Entrepreneurship Day (09 Nov) was marked with a PIB backgrounder showing the startup-scheme architecture. Startup India (2016) has produced over 2.62 lakh DPIIT-recognised startups, up from fewer than 500 in 2014. Its schemes include the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (₹945 crore corpus), the Credit Guarantee Scheme for Startups (cover raised from ₹10 crore to ₹20 crore per borrower) and the Atal Innovation Mission 2.0 (₹2,750 crore up to March 2028). GENESIS has a ₹490 crore outlay over 5 years.
  • PM SVANidhi and PMEGP reached large beneficiary bases. PM SVANidhi (launched June 2020) lends ₹15,000, then ₹25,000, then ₹50,000 to street vendors and has 80.7 lakh+ beneficiaries and ₹20,400 crore+ sanctioned. The Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP) offers a margin-money subsidy of 25% rural / 15% urban for the general category and 35% / 25% for special categories. Project cost limits are ₹50 lakh (manufacturing) and ₹20 lakh (service).
  • ★ The Lakhpati Didi initiative under DAY-NRLM had 3.76 crore Lakhpati Didis by June 2026 and a revised target of 6 crore by March 2029. A Lakhpati Didi is an SHG member whose household earns at least ₹1 lakh annually. The target was 2 crore (15 Aug 2023), raised to 3 crore in the Feb 2024 Interim Budget and to 6 crore in Feb 2026, and 3 crore was reached in Dec 2025. An IIM Ahmedabad assessment found average monthly household income up 61%.
  • The Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA), launched on 15 Aug 2020, has reached over 35.84 crore people. It began in 272 districts, expanded to 372 in Aug 2022 and covered all districts from 15 Aug 2023. It is the flagship awareness arm of the National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction (NAPDDR), whose budget is ₹333 crore for 2026-27 (₹420 crore Revised Estimate for 2025-26). The helpline is 14446, and Article 47 of the Constitution is its constitutional anchor.
  • The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) capped trade margins on 137 anti-cancer drugs, with revised MRPs effective 09 Nov 2026. 679 brands cut prices by more than 50%, with the highest cut at 96.45% and estimated annual consumer savings of ₹3,540.38 crore. The step followed an Expert Committee set up by the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS) on 17 Oct 2026. Consumers can verify prices on the Pharma Sahi Daam app.
  • ★ The Union Budget 2025-26 context appears in fisheries policy: it set an enabling framework for harnessing fisheries from the Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) and High Seas, with emphasis on Lakshadweep and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands. Under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY), investment of ₹218 crore is envisaged for Andaman & Nicobar and ₹175 crore for Lakshadweep, out of ₹39,272 crore committed since 2015. Seafood exports were about ₹73,890 crore in FY 2025-26. The two island groups hold nearly 40% of India's EEZ.
  • Special Campaign 6.0 (2–31 Oct 2026) targeted pendency reduction and cleanliness, with this year's focus on e-waste under the E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022. Indian Railways earned ₹628.49 crore from scrap against a ₹600 crore target, and the Ministry of Power raised ₹15.74 crore. The Ministry of Home Affairs freed 78,125 sq ft and earned over ₹10.17 crore. This is a yearly governance drive and continues the earlier campaigns (5.0 and before).

Economy & Trade

  • ★ India's Balance of Payments (BoP) for September 2026 showed a current account deficit of US$7.4 billion, against US$5.2 billion in September 2025. Merchandise exports were US$45.3 billion and imports US$75.3 billion, giving a trade deficit of US$30.0 billion. Net services were US$19.2 billion and net transfers US$10.9 billion, while net income was -US$7.6 billion. The capital account was +US$16.2 billion, giving an overall balance of +US$8.8 billion (reserves rose).
  • For April–September 2026 the current account deficit was US$25.8 billion (US$18.0 billion a year earlier). Merchandise exports were US$265.3 billion, imports US$441.4 billion and the trade deficit US$176.1 billion, while services net receipts were US$105.0 billion. The capital account was +US$92.6 billion and the overall balance +US$66.8 billion. In September, net Foreign Portfolio Investment was -US$6.7 billion and net Foreign Direct Investment -US$4.9 billion.
  • The Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) Quarterly Bulletin for July–September 2026 (released 10 Nov 2026 by MoSPI) shows improving labour indicators. Overall Labour Force Participation Rate was 55.5% (from 54.6%), rural 58.1% and urban 50.4%, and Female LFPR was 34.7%. Overall unemployment rate was 5.1% (from 5.4%), with rural 4.3% and urban 6.8%. The Worker Population Ratio was 52.7%, and average employment (15+) was 57.9 crore, of which 17.4 crore were female.
  • The Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) moved Lithium-Ion Battery Black Mass exports from "Free" to "Prohibited" (Notification No. 49/2026-27, 10 Nov 2026), effective immediately. It covers ITC(HS) Code 85491400 and follows transitional arrangements under Para 1.05 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023. Black mass contains lithium, cobalt, nickel and manganese, and the goals are domestic recycling feedstock, a circular economy and Aatmanirbhar Bharat. It complements the National Critical Mineral Mission and the ₹1,500 crore Incentive Scheme for Critical Mineral Recycling approved in September 2025.
  • ★ Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal said the US$1 trillion export target is "sacrosanct", with about US$480 billion (48%) achieved in the first six months. He said engineering exports grew about 22%, the 27-nation EU bloc is expected to open at zero duty by March/April, and India is negotiating with 7–8 more countries. The remarks came at the curtain raiser of the Bharat Mobility Global Expo 2027 (4–9 Feb 2027 at Bharat Mandapam and Yashobhoomi), a third edition with 1,750+ exhibitors. Remember that recent FTAs and bilateral deals are a perennial exam theme.
  • Piyush Goyal visited Cairo from 14 Nov 2026, and the 7th India–Egypt Joint Trade Committee met on 15 Nov 2026. Bilateral trade is about US$6.5 billion, with about 70 Indian companies in Egypt investing over US$5 billion, and the visit supports the India–Egypt Strategic Partnership. He co-chaired the meeting with Egypt's Minister of Investment and Foreign Trade, Dr. Mohamed Farid Saleh. Business forums and an Afreximbank meeting were also on the agenda.
  • National Health Accounts (NHA) 2023-24, the 11th round, shows out-of-pocket expenditure falling to 42.7% of total health expenditure (from 64.2% in 2013-14). Government Health Expenditure rose from ₹1,29,778 crore to ₹4,14,196 crore, and per capita government health spending from ₹1,042 to ₹2,959. Social security expenditure on health rose from 6% to 11% of total expenditure, and private health insurance from 3.4% to 10.2%. It uses the System of Health Accounts 2011 framework.
  • India's space economy is about US$9 billion (Aug 2026), with registered space start-ups up from 1 in 2014 to about 440. ISRO launched 399 foreign satellites between 2014 and Jan 2026, against 35 during 1990–2014. FDI is permitted under the automatic route up to 74% in satellite manufacturing and operation, 49% in launch vehicles and spaceports, and 100% in components and subsystems. Projections are about US$44 billion by 2033 and US$100 billion by 2040.
  • TRAI introduced IP-based interconnection port charges (effective 1 Jan 2027) through the Telecommunication Interconnection (Port Charges) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026. Annual ceilings are ₹5.6 lakh for a 1 Gbps IP port and ₹42.3 lakh for a 10 Gbps IP port, and the financial disincentive ceiling is ₹15 lakh per Service Area. The framework supplements the E1/TDM provisions of the 2001 regulation, which was last revised in 2012. The Department of Telecommunications' 4G Saturation Project has cut villages without 4G from 45,180 (2022) to 11,372 (2026).
  • Inland and port logistics indicators improved. Cargo on national waterways rose from 18 million MT (2013-14) to 218 million MT (2025-26), and freight cost per tonne-km is ₹1.06 (waterways), ₹1.41 (rail) and ₹2.28 (road). Visakhapatnam Port handled a record 91.17 million metric tons in 2025-26 (about 10% growth). Cargo rose a further 8.88% from 1 Apr to 31 Oct 2026.

International Affairs & Organizations

  • PM Narendra Modi spoke by phone with Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on 09 Nov 2026. Modi reaffirmed India's human-centric approach and its position that the conflict must end through dialogue and diplomacy. He offered to contribute to all efforts for peaceful resolution and discussed strengthening bilateral cooperation. This is consistent with India's long-standing neutral, dialogue-first position.
  • Minister Kiren Rijiju signed the Bilateral Haj Agreement for Haj-2027 at Jeddah on 09 Nov 2026 with Saudi Minister of Hajj and Umrah Dr. Tawfiq bin Fawzan Al-Rabiah. India's country quota for Haj-2027 is 175,025.   • ★ The first BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting was held in Colombo, chaired by Sri Lanka's Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa, with Union Health Minister J. P. Nadda representing India. The seven participants were Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Thailand, and the meeting adopted a revised BIMSTEC Plan of Action on Public Health (2026–2030). It follows the Sixth BIMSTEC Summit (Bangkok, April 2025). Thailand offered to host the Second meeting in 2028, and meetings will be held every two years.

India's BIMSTEC Centre of Excellence in Traditional Medicine will be hosted at the Institute of Teaching and Research in Ayurveda (ITRA), Jamnagar, Gujarat. Bangladesh proposed a Regional Zoonotic Disease Observatory and Early Warning System, and a BIMSTEC Digital Health Framework will be explored. India also exchanged an MoU for 100 more ambulances for Sri Lanka's 1990 Suwa Seriya service, funded by a grant of SLR 1,725.73 million.
  • UNFCCC COP31 is being held in Antalya, Türkiye, co-led by Türkiye and Australia, under a "COP of Implementation" vision. India's opening statement was delivered by Narendra Bhooshan, Secretary, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC). India stressed equity, Common But Differentiated Responsibilities and Respective Capabilities (CBDR-RC), just transition, adaptation and Article 9.1 of the Paris Agreement on climate finance. It aligned with G77 & China, the Like-Minded Developing Countries (LMDC) and BASIC.
  • At the International Telecommunication Union Plenipotentiary Conference (PP-26) in Doha, Minister of State Dr. Chandra Sekhar Pemmasani presented India's digital story. About 2.2 billion people worldwide are still offline, and India has invested about US$15 billion in BharatNet. He cited 100 5G use-case labs, the Bharat 6G Vision and the World Summit on the Information Society (WSIS) Prize won by Samriddh Gram Centres. Bilateral meetings were held with Malaysia, Iran and Uzbekistan.
  • A dialogue on "Waste to Resource" in the cement and construction sector was held on 10 Nov 2026 by the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) with SINTEF and the Research Council of Norway. It followed the elevation of India–Norway ties to a Green Strategic Partnership during PM Modi's visit to Norway in May. Topics included cement kiln co-processing of hazardous waste (POPs, DDT, PCB) and the 2025 Construction and Demolition Waste Rules. It also referred to India's National Implementation Plan under the Stockholm Convention.
  • Naval and defence diplomacy was busy. INS Surat took part in the 15th Naseem Al Bahr exercise with the Royal Navy of Oman at Muscat, in line with the MAHASAGAR vision. The 8th India–Myanmar Naval Exercise (IMNEX-26) ran at Visakhapatnam from 10–14 Nov, and the inaugural India–Myanmar Tri-Services HADR exercise RAHAT MITRA concluded in Chennai. Army chief Gen Dhiraj Seth visits the Czech Republic from 16–19 Nov 2026.
  • Sea Vigil-26, the 5th Pan-India Coastal Defence Exercise, runs 11–19 Nov 2026. It was conceptualised in 2018 after the 26/11 Mumbai attacks and covers the entire coastline and EEZ, and is a build-up to the Navy's biennial TROPEX. INS Trishul's port call at Safaga, Egypt, drew on the India–Egypt Strategic Partnership (2023) and the Defence Cooperation MoU of Sept 2022.
  • The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) rationalised India's visa regime on 27 Aug 2026, cutting main categories from 13 to 9 and sub-categories from 49 to 23. An earlier round in October 2025 had cut categories from 22 to 13 and sub-categories from 63 to 49. e-Visa is available to nationals of 172 countries under 13 sub-categories. Examples include V-1 (30 days, multiple entries), M-1/M-3 medical visas (one year by default) and e-S1 for the Study in India portal.
  • The Commonwealth Women Parliamentarians (CWP) India Region Conference is set for 17–18 Nov 2026 at Vidhana Soudha, Bengaluru. It will be inaugurated by Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla, and its theme is on inclusive cyberspace and mentorship for new women legislators. It shows India's role in Commonwealth parliamentary forums.

Awards, Rankings & Appointments

  • ★ President Droupadi Murmu conferred the Swachh Survekshan 2025-26 (10th edition) awards at Vigyan Bhawan on 11 Nov 2026. There were 60 national awards, and Swachh River Front Town, Swachh Coastal Town and Clean Himalayan City were new categories. The survey grew from 73 cities in 2016 to 4,751 cities, assessed on 43 indicators across 10 areas by 3,000+ trained assessors. Manohar Lal is the Union Minister for Housing and Urban Affairs (and Power).
  • ★ Swachh Survekshan rankings: the top Swachh Shehar Jodi pairs were Ahmedabad–Sanand, Indore–Depalpur and Tirupati–Kuppam. The Super Swachh League top 3 were Navi Mumbai, Surat and Vijayawada, and the million-plus top 3 were GVMC Visakhapatnam, Jabalpur and Lucknow. Clean Himalayan Cities: Rudrapur, Jammu and Shopian. Swachh River Front Towns: Rewa, Bilaspur and Varanasi, and Swachh Coastal Towns: Puri, Alappuzha and Ratnagiri. Swachh Shehar Jodi (launched 27 Sept 2025) pairs 70 mentor cities with 191 mentee cities.
  • ★ India has the 3rd largest startup ecosystem in the world, with over 2.62 lakh DPIIT-recognised startups. Over 55,200 startups were recognised in FY 2025-26, the highest in a single year since 2016. The leading States (31 Mar 2026) are Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi and Gujarat. About 48% of startups have at least one woman director or partner.
  • India won 2 Gold, 2 Silver and 1 Bronze at the 5th International Olympiad on Astronomy and Astrophysics for Juniors (IOAA-Jr. 2026), at Ubon Ratchathani, Thailand (1–8 Nov 2026). The event had 104 students from 25 countries. Gold went to Siddharth Laxmisha (Puducherry) and Swara Srujal Patel (Gujarat), Silver to Aarna Chaplot and Krish Bhaskar, and Bronze to Akshara Gulab Ambone. The National Council of Science Museums (NCSM) is the Authorized National Representative Organization.
  • Prof. Arun Kumarendu Singh of IIT Kharagpur's School of Medicine was appointed a two-year Advisor on maternal and child healthcare to the West Bengal Health & Family Welfare Department. He is called the "Father of Sick Newborn Care Units (SNCUs)" in India and is associated with the Purulia Model. The other appointee was Dr. Arup Kumar Maji, and the Director of IIT Kharagpur is Prof. Suman Chakraborty.
  • The iBRIC+ Spotlight Scientist of the Year-2026 awards went to Dr Sunil Laxman, Dr Rashna Bhandari and Prof. Bhabatosh Das. They were announced at the 3rd Foundation Day of the Biotechnology Research and Innovation Council (BRIC) on 10 Nov 2026. Dr Rajesh S. Gokhale is Secretary, DBT, and Director General, BRIC, which unifies 13 biotech institutions across 11 cities. Dr Jitendra Singh is Minister of State (Independent Charge) for Science & Technology and Earth Sciences.
  • Key office-holders in the week's news (useful for portfolio questions): Amit Shah is Union Home Minister and Minister of Cooperation, Piyush Goyal is Commerce & Industry Minister and Sarbananda Sonowal is Minister of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. J. P. Nadda is Union Health Minister and Minister of Chemicals and Fertilizers, and Kiren Rijiju is Minister for Parliamentary Affairs and Minority Affairs. Adm Krishna Swaminathan is Chief of the Naval Staff, Gen Dhiraj Seth is Chief of the Army Staff, and Rajesh Kumar Singh is Defence Secretary. C. P. Radhakrishnan is Vice-President.
  • The Ministry of Ports' MV Ganga Vilas holds a Limca Book of Records entry as the world's longest river cruise. It covers 3,200+ km across India and Bangladesh, 2 countries, 5 States and 27 river systems, with 50 tourist destinations. The note appeared in the Yamuna cruise release.

Sports / Environment / Science & Tech

  • Anuj Shrivatri became India's 100th Chess Grandmaster, and PM Modi congratulated him on 10 Nov 2026. The 100-GM milestone marks the depth of Indian chess. The Department of Sports separately held its Special Campaign 6.0 activities (2–31 Oct 2026), including the Fit India Swachhata Freedom Run launched on 02 Oct.
  • ★ Skyroot Aerospace's Vikram-I reached orbit on its first flight (Mission Aagaman) on 18 July 2026, the first orbital launch by an Indian private company from Indian soil. Earlier milestones were Vikram-S (Nov 2022, Mission Prarambh) and Agnikul Cosmos' launch from the private Dhanush pad (May 2024). The institutional timeline is INCOSPAR (1962, under the Department of Atomic Energy), ISRO (August 1969), the Department of Space (June 1972) and NewSpace India Limited (March 2019). IN-SPACe was set up after the 24 June 2020 reforms.
  • Space policy funding instruments include a ₹500 crore Technology Adoption Fund (Feb 2025) and a ₹1,000 crore Venture Capital Fund under IN-SPACe. The IN-SPACe Seed Fund offers up to ₹1 crore per start-up, and the Indian Space Policy 2023 defines the roles of ISRO, IN-SPACe and NSIL. NSIL revenue rose from ₹321.77 crore (FY 2021-22) to over ₹3,000 crore (FY 2024-25). IN-SPACe's Norms, Guidelines and Procedures were issued on 3 May 2024.
  • COSMOS-Mysuru Planetarium is described as India's first LED dome planetarium, built by the Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA) on the University of Mysore campus. It has a 15-metre tilted dome with about 6,150 panels and 4.2 crore LEDs, and shows in Kannada, English and Hindi. It was part of PM Modi's 13–14 Nov Karnataka visit, which included projects worth over ₹15,600 crore.
  • PM Modi's Bengaluru programme included India's largest underground LPG storage cavern at Mangaluru (₹850 crore+, 80,000 MT capacity). It also included a GAIL Compressed Biogas plant at Haralakunte, processing about 300 tonnes of waste daily to produce 12.6 tonnes of CBG. Full electrification of the Bengaluru–Mangaluru railway route and the Bengaluru–Mangaluru–Madgaon Vande Bharat Express were also highlighted. These link to the clean-energy and circular-economy themes.
  • Adi Vaani, launched on 1 September 2025 by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, is an AI-powered translation platform for tribal languages. It was developed by an IIT Delhi-led consortium and works with 13 tribal languages, with text translation currently in nine. It offers 300+ language primers and 24 dictionaries, and AdiKosh is its word bank. The Ministry also launched the AI assistant "Jago" on 24 August 2026.
  • The Biotechnology Research and Innovation Council (BRIC) said India's bioeconomy has grown from about US$10 billion to over US$195 billion. Biotech startups rose from about 50–60 to over 11,000. Biofoundry hubs are at Mohali, Hyderabad, Pune and Thiruvananthapuram, and the Wellcome Trust has granted up to ₹21.7 crore for PhD training.
  • Delhi's first recreational Yamuna River Cruise was inaugurated at Sonia Vihar on 10 Nov 2026 by Union Minister Sarbananda Sonowal. It runs on a 30-seater electric vessel on the Sonia Vihar–Jagatpur stretch (about 6 km round trip). The Yamuna is National Waterway-110 under the National Waterways Act, 2016, and the project is implemented by the Inland Waterways Authority of India (IWAI) with DDA, DTTDC and DJB.
  • The 3rd National Conference on Cyber Security, Digital Forensics & Intelligence (NCCDFI 2026) takes place on 16–17 Nov 2026 at Dr. Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi. It is organised by the National Institute of Electronics & Information Technology (NIELIT) under MeitY, with the theme "Cyber Secure Bharat: Fortifying India's Digital Future". Its Cyber Kushti hackathon drew 1,031 teams, and earlier editions were held at Kohima (2024) and Guwahati (2025).
