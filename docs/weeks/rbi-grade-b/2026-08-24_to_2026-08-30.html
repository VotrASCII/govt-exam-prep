01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • 📌 RBI launched the USD‑INR Swap Facility on 8 June 2026 – the new scheme, aimed at easing foreign‑exchange movements, has already attracted US $73 billion in FX inflows as of 21 August 2026, and US $65.40 billion in FCNR(B) deposits. The initiative, four‑fold larger than the 2013 trial, provides the Reserve Bank of India with a better cushion against price volatility in the foreign‑exchange market.
  • ★ UPI celebrates its 10th anniversary (24 Aug 2026) – the digital wallet platform recorded 24 162 crore transactions and a transaction value of ₹314 lakh crore in FY 2025‑26, a dramatic increase from a single‑digit figure in 2016‑17. Daily average transactions rose to 66 crore, with UPI now accounting for 84 % of India’s digital payments, underscoring the success of the cash‑less push.
  • UPI is operational in 11 overseas countries – Bhutan, Nepal, Singapore, UAE, France, Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Qatar, Greece, and Maldives – allowing cross‑border transactions via the ICC‑1 credit scheme and UPI‑Lite wallet limits, which were raised to ₹10,000 (U P I‑123Pay) and ₹5 000 (U P I‑Lite).
  • RBI announced a new Digital Rupee pilot – the central bank has fast‑tracked a central‑bank‑issued digital currency (CBDC) by enabling a test‐bed on a blockchain platform in collaboration with the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) for a limited scale pilot in mid‑2026.
  • RBI introduced the “Antardrishti” dashboard – a real‑time analytics tool providing the RBI with granular commentary on liquidity, credit, and payment system flows, enabling a proactive stance on financial stability and policy formulation.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • CBN Operation ‘Black Hawk’ (20‑21 Aug 2026) – a coordinated effort by the Central Bureau of Narcotics caught 18.64 kg of crude heroin through interception of a car and a heavy truck on the Sultanpur‑Lucknow Highway, raising the national drug‑trafficking case‑file by 2026.
  • India‑Cambodia trade saw a 36 % jump (FY 25‑26) – bilateral trade between the two nations rose to USD 406.78 million from USD 299 million the previous year, with the April‑June 2026 quarter up 63.25 % YoY, reinforcing Cambodia’s emergence as an 8th‑largest ASEAN partner.
  • India–Morocco trade volume reached USD 4 billion (FY 2025) – reflecting a diversification of Indian exports into refined petroleum, pharmaceuticals, and automobiles under a memorandum of understanding on food safety and cultural exchange.
  • PMFME Conclave 2026 (24–25 Aug 2026) – a 10‑day event at India Habitat Centre that saw 500+ stakeholders from 25 states and UTs, culminating in the launch of a 35 % credit‑linked capital subsidy for small‑scale food processing units and a nationwide “Coffee‑Table Book” of 30 success stories.
  • National Bank (CBN) and PHARMEXCIL MoU – on 24 Aug 2026 Dr Dinesh Bisen and Shri Raja Bhanu signed a voluntary code of conduct to streamline trade facilitation and capacity‑building for genuine pharmaceutical exports.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

  • PM SVANidhi scheme – 12 yr restructuring (27 Aug 2026) – the Ministry of Finance’s flagship financial inclusion programme now offers ₹6,294 crore in disbursements to 10.56 lakh new beneficiaries, up from the original allocation of ₹19,171 cr. Each UPI‑linked RuPay credit card is capped at a ₹30,000 limit, linked to a ₹421 crore interest‑subsidy released to farmers.
  • PMFBY 2026‑27 allocation (29 Aug 2026) – the National Portfolio Scheme, under the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, received ₹12,200 cr for the year. The scheme now covers 241.38 lakh farmers (Kharif 2026) across 278.12 lakh hectares, with claims paid in FY 2025‑26 amounting to ₹9,837.61 cr.
  • Onion buffer‑stock release (26 Aug 2026) – the Ministry of Consumer Affairs and Food Distribution released 2.00 lmt of onions nationwide, setting the retail price at ₹35 /kg. The scheme, overseen by NACF and NAFED, is expected to ease seasonal price spikes during festivals such as Onam, Ganesh Chaturthi, and Diwali.
  • CNG (Compressed Natural Gas) subsidy (29 Aug 2026) – the Government’s fuel‑efficiency drive allocated ₹1,897 MMBtu consumer cost after a 43 % rise from the previous year’s procurement price of ₹1,478 MMBtu, ensuring an accessible price point for end‑users.
  • S3WAAS satellite navigation launch (29 Aug 2026) – the National Informatics Centre rolled out a “Self‑Motioning Intelligent High‑Precision Satellite Variable‑Accuracy Augmentation System” to enhance land‑based navigation for farmers and infrastructure projects, contributing to the broader national monetization pipeline.

Economy & Trade

  • India–Cambodia trade rose to USD 406.78 million (FY 25‑26) – the bilateral trade surge marked by a 36 % YoY increase, with April–June 2026 recording a 63.25 % jump. Cambodia ranked 8th among ASEAN partners, comprising 0.31 % of India‑ASEAN trade.
  • India–Morocco bilateral trade reached USD 4 billion (FY 25) – the trade composition includes refined petroleum, pharmaceuticals, and vehicles, reinforcing the “Cultural Exchange Programme 2026‑2030” under a MoU on food safety signed in August 2026.
  • India–Japan industrial roundtable (Asia‑Pacific) – the 2026 “Japan‑India Year of Shared Horizons” saw Japanese firms such as Mitsubishi, Tokyo Electron, and Mitsubishi Chemical pledge to double to USD 10 billion in capital goods by decade‑end, reflecting a strategic partnership in clean energy and supply‑chain resilience.
  • India–Argentina trade (25 Aug 2026) – the debit side of a growing relationship reached USD 6.5 bn in FY 2025, up 17 % YoY. Focus areas include lithium, energy, agriculture, and pharmaceuticals, with a new Annex I MoU expected for agriculture.
  • India–Uzbekistan trade target – the bilateral trade target of USD 5 billion by 2030 was reaffirmed in late August 2026, with a comprehensive strategic partnership announced during the State Visit.
  • Petroleum exports remain the largest share of India’s merchandise exports (2026) – with crude and refined petroleum products accounting for the majority of total exports, underpinning the energy security strategy.
