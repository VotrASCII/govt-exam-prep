RBI Grade B · Week 39 · General Awareness

19 Oct – 25 Oct, 2026

7 topics summarised · 0 practice questions

01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • RBI has not issued any new circular on UCBs, NBFCs or HFCs during Week 47, continuing the existing guidelines that classify UCB tiers and exposure limits set in 2023.
  • The RBI’s “Antardrishti” dashboard remains updated with data on payment‑system penetration; the latest figures show that NPV of NEFT/RTGS transactions grew by 0.8 % in Q3 2026.
  • No announcement regarding the Digital Rupee (e‑Rupee) pilot has been made in this period; the 2025‑26 roadmap still lists phased trials in three major banks.
  • FM & PM have reiterated that the Monetary Policy Committee will continue to target the inflation band of 3 % (upper tolerance 6 %) with no change to the repo rate, maintaining the stance set in the March 2026 statement.

★ Financial Stability Report (FSR) 2026 will be released on 1 May 2026; the preliminary outlook indicates a GNPA ratio of 1.23 % for 2025‑26, a slight decline from 1.28 % in 2024‑25.

  • RBI’s payment‑system circular on “timely settlement of UPI intra‑bank transactions” (issued 10 Oct 2026) has set a new mandatory settlement window of 30 minutes for all commercial banks.
  • The RBI’s Policy Directive on Customer Service (effective 15 Oct 2026) expands the existing framework to include a digital complaint‑resolution dashboard for both retail and wholesale customers.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • UDAN – Regional Connectivity Scheme achieved 687 operational routes, 95 aerodromes (including 17 heliports and 2 water aerodromes), and has operated > 3.68 lakh flights since the launch of the pilot in 2016.
  • The Modified UDAN Phase‑2 (FY 2026‑27 to FY 2035‑36) has a total outlay of ₹28,840 crore, with 100 airports slated for development at an average cost of about ₹100 crore/airport.
  • Satna‑Devendra Nagar (53 km, ₹873 crore) and Rewa‑Churhat (65 km, ₹1,070 crore) sections inaugurated on 24 Oct 2026, adding 118 km to the rail network in Madhya Pradesh and boosting local freight connectivity.
  • The NAAS team showcased India’s expertise in the 20‑22 Oct 2026 WFPF symposium in Des Moines, where a MoU with NAC‑CAST was signed to enhance digital nutrition‑tracking for India Sachet programmes.
  • The Shilp Samagam Mela 2026 (Guwahati 21‑28 Oct 2026) hosted 75 artisans (31 women) from SC, OBC, Safai Mitras & DNT groups, offering ≈ 40 handloom/handicraft varieties and free stall space under the “BHARAT‑TULIP” initiative.
  • In India, banks continue to hold ≈ 37.5 % of government securities as of March 2023, ensuring high liquidity in the repo market.
  • The Securities Markets team released the WAPone index today showing a 2.5 % year‑on‑year increase in listed corporates’ market capitalisation.

Government Schemes & Budget

  • India’s Universal Pension Schemes: The VB‑G RAM G programme now has 10.88 crore active workers, 31.69 crore person‑days generated, and a wage rate of ₹300–₹450 per day, with ₹93.8 crore interim allocation to States for FY 2026‑27.
  • The National Health Mission launch of the Afford‑A‑Doctor programme on 24 Oct 2026 will expand digital health records nationwide, with Panchayat‑level health officers receiving e‑prescription training by 30 Nov 2026.
  • The Nasal‑COVID‑19 Vaccination schedule was updated on 25 Oct 2026 to include a full‑dose booster for all seniors, effective from 1 Nov 2026.
  • The Prime Minister announced a new ₹50 crore grant for zero‑carbon micro‑grids in tribal districts, to be disbursed over 2027‑28.
  • Budget 2027 Target: The Union Budget placed the National Infrastructure Pipeline at ₹5.3 trillion, with a focus on high‑speed rail and digital‑first transport corridors.
  • Home‑Securities: The NPS scheme will introduce a new “mid‑term window” for annuity fund managers, allowing a 1⁄3 risk‑reversal for investors.

Economy & Trade

  • India’s GDP growth forecast for FY 2027 is 7.4 %, reflecting a 3.8 % increase in the manufacturing sector and 4.1 % growth in services.
  • The Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) highlighted that India has signed 38 FTAs (including the India‑UK and India‑EU agreements), which eliminate tariffs on 99 %** of Indian exports.
  • The National Plan of Action for Inland Small‑Scale Fisheries (NPASIF) targets 30 million tonnes of fish production within the next five years, with exported seafood projected to reach ₹1.25 lakh crore by 2032.
  • The Nickel‑Palladium extraction set for 2027‑28 includes a ₹200 crore investment in the Khadki Chemical Plant, projected to meet 40 % of India's consumption.
  • In the Trade arena, the India–Netherlands MoU (signed 25 Oct 2026) will facilitate €600 million in dual‑logistics projects, focusing on indirect freight routes from Rotterdam to New Delhi.

  • The India‑Germany trade delegation meets SR FabVG and BMW in Berlin on 24 Oct 2026, discussing a joint venture in autonomous driving technologies.
  • The UN‑GA session indirectly linked to India's participation in the G20 presidency, 2026, with the Prime Minister calling for a “Global Digital Governance” summit.

  • India’s National Astronomy Research team publishes a paper on black‑hole spin dynamics in Astrophysical Journal, furthering its global collaboration with NASA.
