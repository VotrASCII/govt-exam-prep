01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • ★ The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) raised the policy repo rate by 25 basis points (bps) to 5.50% and changed its stance to "calibrated tightening". The decision was taken unanimously at the 63rd MPC meeting, held 5–7 October 2026 under Governor Sanjay Malhotra. Under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF), the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate is now 5.25%, while the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate and the Bank Rate are 5.75%. It marks a turn from the easing phase, since the RBI Bulletin notes a cumulative 125 bps repo cut between February 2025 and March 2026.
  • ★ The MPC minutes were published on 21 October 2026 under Section 45ZL of the RBI Act, 1934, which requires release on the fourteenth day after each meeting. The meeting was attended by Governor Sanjay Malhotra, Dr. Nagesh Kumar, Shri Saugata Bhattacharya, Prof. Ram Singh, Dr. Poonam Gupta (Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy) and Shri Indranil Bhattacharyya (Executive Director, nominated by the Central Board). The minutes carry the resolution, each member's vote and each member's statement, and committee composition is a frequent exam question.
  • ★ The MPC projected real Gross Domestic Product (GDP) growth for 2026-27 at 7.1%, with Q2 at 7.2%, Q3 at 6.9% and Q4 at 6.8%. Growth for Q1:2027-28 is projected at 7.1%, and the risks are described as evenly balanced. The projection builds on the National Statistics Office (NSO) estimate of 7.8% real GDP growth in Q1:2026-27, which was higher than expected and was driven by private consumption, fixed investment and services. Risks flagged include a deficient south-west monsoon and strong El Niño conditions.
  • ★ Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation is projected at 5.2% for 2026-27, with Q2 at 4.9%, Q3 at 6.0% and Q4 at 5.7%. Q1:2027-28 is projected at 5.6% and core inflation at 4.4% for 2026-27. CPI inflation rose to 4.8% in August 2026 from 4.5% in July, core inflation rose to 4.2% (2.9% excluding precious metals), and about 37% of the CPI basket recorded inflation above 4%. The flexible inflation targeting band is 4% with a tolerance of ±2%, so the Q3 projection of 6.0% touches the upper tolerance limit.
  • The MPC cited a challenging global backdrop built around the re-escalation of the West Asia conflict and volatile crude oil prices. The US Fed hiked by 25 bps in September, other major central banks tightened, and global bond yields are at record highs on fiscal sustainability concerns. The Committee also named elevated AI-related asset valuations and high public debt as downside risks. These are the stated reasons for recalibrating the domestic policy rate.
  • The RBI ran a series of Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auctions under the LAF to manage near-term liquidity. The 7-day VRR on 19 October notified ₹1,00,000 crore, drew bids of ₹1,50,735 crore, allotted ₹1,00,014 crore and cleared at a cut-off of 5.51%. The overnight VRRs on 21 and 22 October cleared at 5.52% and 5.54%, and the 3-day VRR on 23 October (bids ₹1,06,949 crore) cleared at 5.58% with a weighted average rate of 5.60%. A 4-day VRR of ₹1,50,000 crore was announced for 26 October with reversal on 30 October.
  • The Government of India (GoI) re-issued two dated securities, 6.53% GS 2031 (₹19,000 crore) and 7.71% GS 2066 (₹14,000 crore), at an auction on 23 October 2026. The cut-off prices were ₹96.65 (yield 7.3589%) and ₹97.30 (yield 7.9339%), and there was no devolvement on Primary Dealers. In the underwriting auction the Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) commission cut-offs were 0.69 and 0.94 paise per ₹100. The auction ran on the RBI's Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber) system using the multiple price method, with non-competitive allocation capped at 5% of the notified amount.
  • ★ Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the RBI's Data Centre and Cybersecurity Training Institute in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, on 25 October 2026. Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said the Data Centre will house mission-critical systems including Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) and National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT). The Training Institute will build expertise in cybersecurity, AI and other emerging technologies. This continues the RBI's push for resilient payment infrastructure and indigenous technological capability.
  • The October 2026 RBI Bulletin carried two speeches and four articles. The articles covered the State of the Economy, transmission to lending rates of scheduled commercial banks (SCBs), the NBFC sector review for 2025, and nowcasting of state-level activity using night-time lights and Google Trends. It found that the weighted average lending rate (WALR) on fresh loans fell 93 bps against the 125 bps repo cut, split into an interest rate effect of 80 bps and a composition effect of 13 bps (February 2025 to March 2026). The Bulletin also says the headline CPI edged up in September and that strong inflows under the FCNR(B) swap window boosted reserves.
  • Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) 2018-19 Series-II was finally redeemed on 23 October 2026 at ₹14,425 per unit. SGBs are repayable on expiry of eight years from issue, and this tranche was issued on 23 October 2018. The redemption price is the simple average of the closing price of 999-purity gold of the previous three business days (19, 21 and 22 October 2026) as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA).
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • ★ The RBI issued the Commercial Banks (Forthcoming Instructions) Second Amendment Directions, 2026 on minimum capital requirements for operational risk on 23 October 2026. It specifies the implementation date for the new Basel III Standardised Approach and gives an indicative mapping of Business Indicator components. It follows the 28 November 2025 Directions and the Governor's 1 October 2025 statement that the revised Basel III capital adequacy framework applies from 1 April 2027 to commercial banks, excluding Small Finance Banks, Payment Banks and Regional Rural Banks.
  • ★ The RBI issued the Commercial Banks (Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Eleventh Amendment Directions, 2026 to implement the Basel Committee's Leverage Ratio 2017 Standard. The draft was issued on 7 August 2026 with feedback invited until 28 August 2026, and the final Directions take effect from 1 April 2027. This is a non-risk-based backstop to risk-weighted capital norms.
  • ★ The RBI imposed Directions under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 on Puducherry Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd (an Urban Co-operative Bank, UCB). The Directive is dated 19 October 2026 and applies from the close of business on 21 October 2026 for six months, subject to review. Withdrawals are capped at ₹5,000 per depositor, and eligible depositors get deposit insurance up to ₹5,00,000 from the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC). The RBI stressed that this is not a cancellation of the banking licence.
  • ★ The RBI extended its Directions on two other UCBs, each by three months. The Suri Friends' Union Co-operative Bank Ltd (Suri, West Bengal) is extended from 22 October 2026 to 22 January 2027, and The Konark Urban Co-operative Bank Ltd (Ulhasnagar) from 23 October 2026 to 23 January 2027. The RBI states that such extensions do not imply satisfaction with the bank's financial position. Supervisory Directions on UCBs are a recurring exam topic.
  • ★ The RBI Bulletin's review of the NBFC sector for 2025 found double-digit year-on-year credit growth at end-December 2025. The share of bank borrowings in funding rose moderately, indicating continued concentration in the funding profile. Asset quality has improved, with NPA (non-performing asset) ratios declining consistently in recent years. Text-mining also shows NBFCs increasingly orienting towards AI for customer applications, governance, digital infrastructure and analytics.
  • State Governments were scheduled to raise ₹23,300 crore through State Government Securities (SGS) auctions on 27 October 2026 via E-Kuber. The 13 states are Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Keralam, Manipur, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand. SGS investments count as eligible government securities for Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) purposes under Section 24 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, and interest on new stock is paid half-yearly on April 28 and October 28. The 19 October auction had notified ₹24,400 crore and allotted ₹23,016.418 crore, with Andhra Pradesh accepting nothing and Punjab accepting a partial ₹316.418 crore.
  • The RBI announced a Treasury Bill auction of ₹23,000 crore for 28 October 2026 (91-day ₹8,000 crore, 182-day ₹8,000 crore, 364-day ₹7,000 crore), settling on 29 October. The auction is price based using the multiple price method, and retail non-competitive allocation is capped at 5% of the notified amount. In the previous auction results released on 21 October 2026, the amounts accepted were shown as NIL for all three tenors.
  • The Weekly Statistical Supplement (23 October 2026) showed SCB aggregate deposits of ₹2,78,75,910 crore and bank credit of ₹2,27,42,777 crore as on 15 October 2026. Year-on-year growth was 16.7% for deposits and 18.2% for bank credit, while broad money (M3) grew 16.0%. Under the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025, a fortnight is now the 15th and last calendar day of a month, effective 15 December 2025.
  • The Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC) held its 30th meeting on 23 October 2026. It discussed macro-financial stability, the AI boom, rising global bond yields, tokenisation and cyber resilience. No numerical metrics were released.
  • The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) and Bank of India signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 22 October 2026. It covers quantum financial innovation, quantum-safe technologies, blockchain, AI and talent development in the banking ecosystem.
  • Sagarmala Finance reported sanctioned financing of ₹11,563 crore and disbursements of ₹5,107 crore as of 30 September 2026. Its target is cumulative disbursements of ₹60,205 crore by FY 2031. In the Maritime Development Fund, SBI Ventures is Fund Manager for the MIF component, with Alternative Investment Fund registration due by end-November 2026 and a first close of ₹5,000 crore targeted for January 2027. The Interest Incentivisation Fund (portal www.iifmdf.in) has seen ₹4,500 crore of loans and ₹25 crore of interest subvention released, and government contribution through SMFCL (Sagarmala Finance Corporation Limited) is up to ₹9,800 crore.
  • The Prime Minister cited financial inclusion statistics at the Bhubaneswar RBI event. He mentioned 13 crore first-time entrepreneurs under Mudra Yojana and 9 crore covered by Atal Pension Yojana (38 lakh in Odisha). PM Suraksha Bima Yojana offers a ₹2 lakh cover for ₹20 a year, and PM Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana costs up to ₹450 a year, with ₹25,000 crore of claims disbursed across the two. He also said 1.75 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandirs have been set up in villages.
  • The RBI conducted a Conversion/Switch Auction of GoI securities on 19 October 2026. Source securities (such as 8.26% GS 2027 and 7.26% GS 2029) were switched into longer destination securities (such as 7.19% GS 2060 and 7.41% GS 2036). The 7.50% GS 2034 switch received no offers. Such switches help the government smooth its redemption profile.

Government Schemes & Budget

  • ★ The Ude Desh Ka Aam Nagrik (UDAN) Regional Connectivity Scheme completed 10 years since its launch on 21 October 2016. As of 15 October 2026 it has 687 regional routes, 95 aerodromes (including 17 heliports and 2 water aerodromes), over 3.68 lakh flights and 1.72 crore passengers carried. ₹5,091.23 crore was disbursed as Viability Gap Funding (VGF), and the North-East has 90 operational routes. The pilot flight was Shimla–Delhi in April 2017.
  • ★ The Modified UDAN, approved on 4 July 2026 for FY 2026-27 to FY 2035-36, carries a total outlay of ₹28,840 crore. It provides ₹12,159 crore over 8 years to develop 100 airports and ₹2,577 crore of operations and maintenance support for about 441 aerodromes. VGF for airlines is ₹10,043 crore over 10 years (tapered after 3 years, with 3-year exclusivity), and ₹400 crore backs indigenous aircraft (2 HAL Dhruv for Pawan Hans and 2 HAL Dornier for Alliance Air). It continues and revises the 2016 scheme with a stronger infrastructure and helipad component.
  • ★ The Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) (VB-G RAM G) Act, 2025 came into force on 1 July 2026. It provides a statutory guarantee of 125 days of wage employment per fiscal year per rural household, with 318 permissible work types across four themes: water security, core rural infrastructure, livelihood-related infrastructure and extreme-weather mitigation. As of 24 September 2026, women's participation is 61.65%, over 31.69 crore person-days have been generated, the wage rate is ₹300–₹450 per day, and 15.17 crore Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards are issued. The interim allocation for 2026-27 is ₹93,841 crore, and a separate release cites ₹95,692 crore, so briefings vary slightly.
  • ★ India created 11 crore unique digital Farmer IDs by the night of 23 October 2026, five months ahead of the 31 March 2027 deadline. The IDs are linked to the Digital Agriculture Mission (approved 2 September 2024) and support faster bank loans, Kisan Credit Card access, direct credit of crop insurance compensation, disaster relief and Minimum Support Price (MSP) procurement. More than 6 crore farmers have already received direct benefits through the Farmer-ID system. Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan credited cooperative federalism.
  • The Khet Bachao Abhiyan – Rabi 2026 was launched at Karnal, Haryana on 22 October 2026 and runs until 30 November 2026. It promotes balanced fertiliser use, crop diversification, natural farming and crop-residue management. It targets about 1 crore participants through ATMA (Agricultural Technology Management Agency) and ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) channels, with 4,150 teams of Krishi Vigyan Kendras and ATMA staff.
  • ★ The Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan (PM-KUSUM), launched in February 2019, has about ₹34,422 crore of central financial assistance and 30 lakh solar-connected pumps. At the 7th AgriVoltaics World Conference (21–23 October 2026, Bharat Mandapam), the Agriculture Minister said its next phase would launch the "world's most ambitious AgriVoltaics programme". About 47.5 million hectares are suitable for agrivoltaics, and states such as Maharashtra (Mukhyamantri Saur Krushi Vahini Yojana 2.0), Gujarat (Kisan Suryoday Yojana) and Karnataka (Surya Raitha) have their own schemes.
  • ★ The 2nd National Urban Conclave (Bhubaneswar, 24–25 October 2026) highlighted several Budget-linked urban financing figures. These were an Urban Challenge Fund of ₹1 lakh crore, a Credit Repayment Guarantee Scheme of ₹5,000 crore, an Urbanisation Premium Grant of ₹10,000 crore, and ₹5,000 crore each for 7 City Economic Regions over 5 years. Launches included HUDCO's Urban Invest Window (UiWIN), the Samarthya platform, an updated PMAY-U 2.0 mobile app and the e-book "Mera Ghar Mera Abhimaan". Its theme was "Urban Rising – Rethinking Urban Systems, Solutions & Delivery".
  • At the 19th Urban Mobility India Conference (23–24 October 2026, Bhubaneswar), the operational metro network was put at 1,185 km across 26 cities. Since December 2025, 9 metro projects (about 174 km) worth over ₹59,000 crore have been approved, and about 1,100 electric buses run in 28 cities. The theme was "Urban Accessibility and Liveability in Digital Age", and it is a good source for infrastructure figures.
  • Namami Gange comprises 219 projects worth ₹35,664 crore, of which 147 are completed, with 6,603 MLD of total sewage treatment plant (STP) capacity. Under the Hybrid Annuity Model, 31 projects (1,955 MLD) have been taken up, of which 11 (454 MLD) are completed. Technologies include NEREDA and electrocoagulation.
  • Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0 held a National Training of Trainers at India Habitat Centre, New Delhi. It offers 8 story-based modules and aims to reach about 54 lakh stakeholders, with an emphasis on community ownership and digital governance.
  • Shilp Samagam Mela 2026 is an artisan marketing initiative of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment with NSFDC, NBCFDC and NSKFDC. The Mysuru edition (11–19 October 2026) was the first of FY 2026-27, with 83 stalls. The Guwahati edition (Farm Gate, Khanapara, ending 28 October 2026) has 75 artisans (31 women) and about 40 product varieties. Both promote BHARAT-TULIP, the Traditional Artisans' Upliftment Livelihood Programme, for SC, OBC, Safai Mitra and Denotified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribe artisans.
  • SARAS Mela 2026 at Punnami Ghat, Vijayawada (13–31 October 2026) is the first National SARAS Mela outside Delhi-NCR. It is run under Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM), with about 1,500 women entrepreneurs, over 650 stalls and 34 States/UTs. The Lakhpati Didi target was enhanced to 6 crore (announced February 2026) after the 3 crore milestone in December 2025.
  • The new Satna–Devendranagar (53 km, ₹873 crore) and Rewa–Churhat (65 km, ₹1,070 crore) rail sections were inaugurated, adding 118 km. They are part of the 541 km Lalitpur–Singrauli project and give Sidhi district its first rail connection since Independence. Madhya Pradesh's railway budget for FY 2026-27 is ₹15,188 crore.
  • Operation Trident struck 38 locations across Assam, Mizoram and Manipur on 23 October 2026, uncovering ₹100 crore in hawala transactions. About 1,424 bank accounts were frozen, properties worth ₹17.82 crore attached and 18 people arrested. It reflects a shift to a network-centric, financial-disruption approach under the Vision Document on Narcotics Control 2026-2029, with the goal of eradicating drug networks by 31 December 2029.

Economy & Trade

  • ★ The Index of Core Industries (ICI) grew 7.9% in September 2026 (provisional) against 5.9% in August 2026 (final). August growth was revised up from 4.8%, and cumulative April–September 2026 growth was 5.1% against 2.5% a year earlier. Iron Ore led at 18.4%, followed by Electricity (11.4%), Coal (10.7%), Refinery Products (10.3%), Cement (9.8%) and Steel (4.9%), while Fertilizers, Natural Gas and Crude Oil contracted.
  • ★ The India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement (FTA) came into effect on 20 October 2026. On 19 October the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC), represented by Chairman Vivek Chaturvedi, signed a Memorandum of Cooperation (MoC) with the New Zealand Customs Service (Chief Executive Christine Stevenson) for electronic exchange of origin data. This improves trade facilitation and preference utilisation. It is an addition to India's recent bilateral deals, alongside the India–UK and India–EU agreements.
  • ★ India's total foreign exchange reserves stood at US$721,016 million (₹68,46,160 crore) on 16 October 2026, down US$5,011 million over the week. The components were Foreign Currency Assets US$591,847 million, Gold US$105,800 million, SDRs US$18,578 million and the Reserve Position in the IMF US$4,791 million. The RBI Bulletin reported strong FCNR(B) swap inflows adding to reserves.
  • Engineering goods exports crossed US$70 billion in the first half of FY 2026-27, about 20% year-on-year growth, against a target of US$250 billion by 2030. This was discussed at a Chintan Shivir, which covered quality raw materials, Quality Control Orders (QCOs) and sustainability-linked trade measures. The RBI Bulletin separately notes double-digit merchandise export growth in the first six months of 2026-27, narrowing the trade deficit.
  • Defence Minister Rajnath Singh's address to the diaspora in Dushanbe cited several macro figures. These were a current account deficit of 0.61% of GDP last year, forex reserves above US$700 billion, 38 FTAs, and record defence exports of ₹38,424 crore and production of ₹1.80 lakh crore. He said India contributes more than 16% of global growth, renewable capacity grew from 75 GW (2014) to about 300 GW (2026), and UPI value rose from ₹7,000 crore (2016-17) to ₹314 lakh crore (2025-26). At the Army Commanders' Conference he gave 2025-26 production as ₹1.78 lakh crore and exports as about ₹38,000 crore.
  • India's fish production reached 197.75 lakh MT in 2024-25, with inland fisheries contributing 151.60 lakh MT (about 75%). Seafood exports in 2025-26 were ₹73,890 crore. The Fisheries Investors & Exporters Conclave in Bhopal (23 October 2026) highlighted Madhya Pradesh output rising from 2.00 lakh MT (2019-20) to 4.45 lakh MT (2024-25) under the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY), and a national plan targets 30 million tonnes in five years.
  • Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal visited Germany (22–24 October) and the Netherlands (24–27 October) with a delegation of about 100 businesses and start-ups. He attended the Berlin Global Dialogue, met German Federal Minister Katherina Reiche, and inaugurated the India Centre at DGAP. He then visited the Port of Rotterdam on 25 October. The focus was India–EU trade ties, advanced manufacturing and maritime logistics.
  • The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) seized about 46,920 kg of Pakistani-origin watermelon and muskmelon seeds under Operation Deep Manifest at Nhava Sheva Port. The consignments were misdeclared as UAE origin and were valued at about ₹2.02 crore. They violated DGFT (Directorate General of Foreign Trade) Notification 06/2025-26 banning goods from Pakistan and Notification 02/2023 on seed prohibition.
  • India Chem 2026 (14th biennial edition, Mumbai, 22–24 October 2026) was themed "Advantage Bharat: An Emerging Global Chemicals Hub". It was organised by the Department of Chemicals & Petrochemicals and FICCI, and inaugurated by Union Minister JP Nadda. India is the 6th-largest chemicals producer globally and 4th in Asia, and a USD 1 trillion industry is projected by 2040.
  • The Consumer Protection authority, the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA), penalised sellers and platforms over walkie-talkie listings. The cumulative penalties were ₹58 lakh on 12 entities, including ₹10 lakh each on Amazon Seller Services, Flipkart Internet, Meesho, Meta Platforms and TradeIndia, and ₹1 lakh on Hogar Technologies & Innovations LLP. There were 16,970 non-compliant listings, and some orders are under challenge in the Karnataka and Delhi High Courts and the NCDRC.

International Affairs & Organizations

India called for unified action to dismantle the entire ecosystem of terrorism. It also pushed an Action Plan for Multilateral Trade and Economic Cooperation (2026-2030), open supply chains, and connectivity through the International North–South Transport Corridor and Chabahar Port.

  • The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) will showcase India's Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) outcomes at a side-event of the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) COP 17 on 28 October 2026 in Yerevan, Armenia. The announcement concerns ₹1,03,63,188 disbursed to 27 State Biodiversity Boards and 2 Union Territory Biodiversity Councils.   • The Ramsar Convention was adopted on 2 February 1971 and entered into force in 1975, and India became a party on 1 February 1982. It supports CAQM and GRAP and is part of Mission Mausam.
