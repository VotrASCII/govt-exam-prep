02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • The Central Provident Fund (EPFO) raised the statutory wage ceiling to ₹25,000 per month, effective from 17 Sep 2026, broadening the coverage of the Employees’ Provident Fund and its associated benefits for a larger worker cohort.
  • The National Single Window System (NSWS) hit its five‑year milestone on 22 Sep 2026, having processed over 2.26 lakh approvals annually and housed more than 5.69 lakh business entities. The platform’s integration with 327 Central and 3,452 State portals enhances digitised approvals for new businesses across India.
  • The banks were permitted to open on Sundays from 27 Sep 2026, following RBI approval. Public and regional rural banks now operate seven days a week to mitigate disruptions from proposed strikes, thereby ensuring uninterrupted payments and customer service.
  • The UDAN‑2026 scheme progressed significantly, with 687 routes and 95 aerodromes operational at launch, and 1.71 crore passengers having flown on the Challenge‑Mode airport development portal. The programme has also created an estimated 7.7 million aviation‑sector jobs, with prospects for 25 million by 2040.
  • The Special Campaign 6.0 began operations on 2 Oct 2026, focusing on cleanliness, e‑waste segregation, space utilisation and grievance redressal across public sector enterprises and attached organisations.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

  • The Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM‑JAY) marked its eighth anniversary on 22 Sep 2026, having covered 13.25 crore admissions worth ₹2.03 lakh crore at over 38,000 empanelled hospitals by the end of August 2026. The scheme continues to deliver cashless healthcare to a 33 % share of the population.
  • The National Bay of Bengal Infrastructure Development Project operated under special campaigns in 2026; cleanliness drives under “Swachhata Hi Seva” began on 17 Sep 2026 across the Ministry of Fertilizers’ units, with daily reporting to the SHS‑2026 portal.
  • The National Ayush Mission released the “Ayush Services Sector Report” in Nagpur on 23 Sep 2026, comprising safety dossiers for Gokshura and Chandrasura, standard treatment guidelines for skin disorders, and the CCRAS‑AGNI assessment scale for clinical evaluation. The report emphasizes integration of AYUSH with mainstream health to reduce NCD burden by 2027.
  • The Government’s “Special Campaign 6” intensified efforts for public grievances, with 800 appeals to be disposed under the CRGMP during the October campaign, complementing the previous year’s removal of 6,500 physical files and 653 electronic files.
04

Economy & Trade

  • Gold smuggling seizures by the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) in Mumbai captured 21 kg of foreign‑origin gold worth over ₹61 crore across seven operations, while the Assam‑based seizures hit 12 kg worth ₹17 crore in inter‑border toll checkpoints. The successful interdictions underscore the effectiveness of customs controls and the “Mani‑Vidhana” initiatives.
  • The 9th India International Water Week (IIWW 2026) convened from 22‑26 Sep 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, under the theme “Climate‑Resilient Water Management.” It announced the Sustainable Urban River Management framework and AI/ML‑based reservoir sediment management, offering technical guidelines for 227 delegates across 14 partner states and 2 partner countries.
  • The Special Campaign 6.0 IL gave priority to e‑waste disposal where 4,792 physical files and 1,275 electronic files were examined, with 237 physical and 170 electronic files closed, resulting in a 7,156 sq ft space audit and strengthening the integrity of departmental records.

International Affairs & Organizations

  • The 9th International Water Week (IIWW 2026) featured 7,224 delegates, 227 exhibitors and 119 paper presentations, with the theme “Climate‑Resilient Water Management.” The event broadcasted insights from partner ministries such as the Jal Shakti Ministry, affirming India’s leadership in water security.
  • India’s BRICS tax delegation held a meeting on 23 Sep 2026, forming two working groups for International Taxation & Transfer Pricing and Revenue Statistics, with China confirmed as chair for 2027. This move underpins India’s growing influence within the BRICS economic bloc.
  • The European Union’s “IN‑STEP” delegation toured India on 25 Sep 2026, with 38 global‑south participants exploring technology, AI, and climate cooperation, thereby reinforcing India‑EU strategic collaboration.
  • The 5th IN‑STEP (International Strategic Engagement Programme) concluded on 25 Sep 2026, reinforcing India’s role in the Global South through 22 countries and 38 delegates.
