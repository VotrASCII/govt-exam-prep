RBI & Monetary Policy

  • RBI issued no new circulars on UCB exposure limits during Week 37 – the central bank chose to hold its policy stance steady as it monitors the medium‑term impact of the ongoing fiscal stimulus.
  • The RBI’s Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) refrained from altering the repo‑rate for the month of August – keeping the policy rate unchanged to focus on maintaining the inflation‑target corridor.
  • RBI Digital Rupee pilots transferred from the earlier “e‑Rupee wallet” to a feeder‑bank secure platform – helping the RBI seed wider DBT‑enabled transactions across state programmes.
  • The RBI reaffirmed its stance on the payment‑system framework for RTGS, NEFT and UPI – it clarified that the 24‑hour settlement window for RTGS transactions will remain at 800 seconds, while UPI will continue to be free of transaction charges.
  • The RBI announced a new advisory on the risk‑adjusted capital cushion for banks with RWA in the 4‑5 % range – aiming to enhance resilience against potential credit downgrades amid a slowing commercial‑credit environment.
