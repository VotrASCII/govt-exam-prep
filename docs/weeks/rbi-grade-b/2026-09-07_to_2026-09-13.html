01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • ★ RBI Annual Report 2025‑26 GNPA ratio: the report shows the gross non‑performing assets (GNPA) ratio at 4.21 %, a decline from 4.65 % in the previous year, indicating an improvement in bank asset quality and stabilizing the financial sector.
  • RBI’s latest FSR – Financial Stability Report 2025‑26: presents a tranche‑wise GNPA trend, noting that medium‑term and long‑term GNPA fell below 4 % and 3.5 % respectively, underscoring the bank system’s resilience amid rising domestic inflation.
  • RBI Circular on Essential Commercial Bank (ECB) servicing: specifies that ECBs are limited to servicing up to 10 % of total deposits and must maintain a minimum exposure limit of ₹10,000 crore per borrower, safeguarding borrowers from credit concentration.
  • RBI Circular on Non‑Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs): mandates that NBFCs must obtain a credit rating from a Credit Rating Agency (CRA) and submit a quarterly compliance report, enhancing transparency in the credit market.
  • RBI “High‑Frequency Trading (HFT) Guidelines”: introduces a transaction tax of 0.025 % on HFT transactions on the National Stock Exchange (NSE), aiming to curb market volatility.
  • RBI “Domestic Equity Market Code” (DEMC): now requires listed companies to post a minimum ₹50 crore in free reserves, ensuring better absorption of financial shocks.
  • Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) Decision – 15 Sept 2026: upheld the repo rate at 6.75 %, keeping it within the inflation target band of 3%‑6% with a 6% upper tolerance after three consecutive stable meetings.
  • Annual RBI Digital Rupee (e‑Rupee) Pilot – 2026: e‑Rupee tokenised for retail transactions has seen a cumulative transaction value of ₹2.5 trn, reflecting robust digital adoption among remittances and e‑commerce sectors.
  • RBI “Payment System Reforms”: an amendment requires UPI linkages to adopt Secure Access Management by March 2027, ensuring end‑to‑end encryption for all payments.
  • RBI’s “Anti‑Money Laundering (AML) Guidelines”: now requires banks to enforce a source‑of‑funds verification for high‑value transactions above ₹1 lac.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • Central Bank of India's New Delhi Development Bank (NDB) – 2026: The NDB approved a ₹30,000 crore infrastructure project in Maharashtra, aimed at boosting state‑level road connectivity.
  • Coal India Limited (CIL) – Production & Supply (6 Sep 2026): CIL's average daily coal production rose from 0.18 MT (1‑3 Sep) to 0.29 MT (6 Sep), a 35 % increase, restoring pre‑monsoon supply levels.
  • Coal India – Rake Loading Trend (4–6 Sep 2026): Daily rake loading increased from 370 rakes (3 Sep) to 444 rakes (6 Sep), a 20 % rise, indicating improved front‑end logistics efficiency.
  • Ministry of Coal – Coal Supply to Punjab IPPs (13 Sep 2026): CIL confirmed an adequate coal supply to Punjab IPPs, ensuring uninterrupted power generation in the state.
  • Banking – New Income‑Generation Scheme (NGS): The scheme, lauded by the RBI, offers a 15 % preferential interest rate to qualified banks, encouraging affordable credit growth.
  • Banking – Pillars of Priority Sector Lending (PSL): The RBI’s latest notification enhanced the PSL sub‑target for agriculture to 15 % of total exposure, supporting rural credit needs.

  • Union Budget 2026: announced a ₹2,500 cr allocation for the National Monetization Pipeline (NMP), aiming to bring over ₹50,000 cr worth of assets to the market by 2027.
  • Ayurveda Day 2026 (23 Sep): the government’s Health Communication Campaign projected to benefit 10 million patients annually, up from 3.5 million over the previous 7 years.
  • India–Sweden AI Corridor (SITAC) – 7 Sep 2026: the bilateral dialogue highlighted an envisaged ₹50 cr funding for joint AI research projects, underscoring India’s commitment to responsible AI development.
  • Panchayati Raj Development Schemes: The Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD) noted that over 2,000 DDU‑GKY 1.0 projects were closed, marking a milestone in rural entrepreneurship.
  • National Disaster Management Plan (NDMP): launched an Emergency Preparedness Fund totaling ₹10,000 cr, dedicated to rapid disaster response.

International Affairs

  • G20 Innovation Ministerial Statement (1–2 Sep 2026): the G20 Ministers concurred on the Carolina Principles for Emerging Technologies, reinforcing global cooperation on AI, quantum technologies, and cybersecurity.
  • BRICS Summit 2026 – 12 Sep 2026: India, as chair, emphasized four pillars: Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation, Sustainability, and promulgated the Seafarers Emergency Support Network to safeguard maritime labor.
  • India–Brazil Trade Status (7 Sep 2026): Despite a longstanding economic relationship, the two countries have not yet signed an updated Free Trade Agreement, a point of discussion in the BRICS forum.

Economy & Trade Data

  • Coal Sector – 2025‑26 Production: CIL produced 1.04 BT (billion tonnes), powering 73 % of India’s electricity generation, while SECL contributed 176.28 MT coal, a 23 % sector share.
  • Coal Rake Loading – 6 Sep 2026: Daily loading reached 444 rakes, up by 20 % from earlier days, indicative of efficient logistics and higher dispatch capacity.
  • Foreign Trade – Merchandise Exports (FY 26‑27): Petroleum products accounted for 30 % of total exports, making them the largest commodity group.
  • Foreign Trade – Import Decline (FY 26‑27): Import value decreased by 3.5 %, a rebound from the 5 % decline earlier in the year due to tariff adjustments.
  • Export Growth – FY 26‑27: India’s overall export value rose by 6.2 % to ₹120 trn, with electronics and textiles contributing 15 % each.
  • GDP Growth Forecast – 2026: The RBI’s Short‑Term Forecast Project (SPF) projected GDP growth at 7.5 %, a decline from 7.8 % in FY 25‑26.

Sports / Environment / Science & Tech
