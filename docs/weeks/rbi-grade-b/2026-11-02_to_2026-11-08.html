01

RBI & Monetary Policy

Bharat and India do not make up a wide gregarious distribution that was announced in the September press releases.

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) continued to not release any new circulars or statistical data in this week, and therefore, no official monetary‑policy developments or foreign‑exchange‑rate particulars are reported in the available press releases for this period.

02

Banking & Financial Sector

The mining sector is closely linked with the financial framework of the country, and this week it recorded a plethora of data points and award recognitions that are now needed for future investment decisions.

RBI release for this period does not list new banking regulations, so the following information is extracted from the health, coal, and infrastructure related releases.

  • The Coal Star Rating Awards (FY 2024‑25) on 02 Nov 2026 recognised five mines that achieved Five‑Star ratings, and ten mines clinched the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd positions with scores above 93 % (National Coal Development Corporation Limited and NTPC’s opencast segment receiving the highest scores).
  • Western Coalfields Limited (WCL) recorded a production of 32.96 MT, a 15.92 % increase over the previous accounting period, and an offtake of 41.56 MT, a 25.54 % rise. During October 2026 alone WCL produced 5.93 MT (up 64.87 %) and off‑took 6.67 MT (up 50.16 %).
  • The same October report shows WCL achieved 108.60 % of the monthly production target and 102.40 % of the monthly offtake target; meanwhile 104.56 % of the whole‑month offtake target was surpassed.
  • WCL’s stock liquidation fell from 13.034 MT, 1 April 2026, to 4.44 MT, 31 Oct 2026, thereby liquidating 8.6 MT (nearly 66 %) of its existing reservation.
  • The mine meanwhile maintained a rake‑loading of 6,146 rakes by the end of the month, an increase of 39.6 % over the 2025 tally.
  • 87 operational mines were counted in the April‑to‑October 2026 period after the addition of Lohari mine in Jharkhand earlier this month, bringing the national total to 87.
  • Dynamic Demand: 1.5 × 10² MT of monthly coal demand is being met from many iron‑ore driven sectors, indicating an era of rising furnace loads and metallurgical demands across eastern and central India.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

India’s flagship programmes for clean‑energy, rural housing, and digital governance are pushing forward, with several schemes receiving fresh funding, approvals, or inaugurations in this week.

  • The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana launched in February 2024 has now benefited ≈ 65 lakh households within 24–25 months, achieving a savings of about 76 % on electricity bills and enabling zero‑bill households to sell surplus power.
  • The PM KUSUM policy has launched to benefit more than 30 lakh farmers, each saving an estimated ₹1 lakh per year via solar‑integrated irrigation systems, and it is rapidly expanding to cover over 40,000 MW of net photovoltaic capacity.
  • The PM Surya Sarovar Yojana has an outlay of > 5,000 crore rupees and aims to install 5,000 MW of floating solar capacity across reservoirs, ponds, and lakes.
  • The Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 set an outlay of > 1 lakh crore for green energy corridors, targeting ₹2 lakh crore of investment.
  • Newly inducted ₹53,866.50 crore contracts between the Ministry of Defence and GRSE & GSL to construct 8 Next‑Generation Corvettes will generate ≈ 72 lakh person‑days in shipyards and ≈ 460 lakh person‑days in ancillary MSMEs.
  • Digital Life Certificate (DLC) campaign 5 was launched on 06 Nov 2026, targeting > 2 crore certificates nationwide, which will be produced via face‑authentication technology under the Jeevan Pramaan app.
  • Advanced Radiotherapy Facility at AIIMS Rajkot was inaugurated on 02 Nov 2026 with an investment of ≈ ₹39 crore and a capacity of 600–700 cases per year for oncologic treatments.
  • The PM Surya Ghar programme recorded a total installation of ≈ 100 k units every six days, echoing a growth of 3 GW in 10 years to now exceed 172 GW installed capacity.
04

Economy & Trade

India’s commodity and services exports dominated in October 2026, marking a decade‑long record for the country.

  • In October 2026, 87 active coal mines pumped a gross production of 105.33 MT and a gross dispatch of 111.95 MT, indicating a decrease of 11.3 % YoY on the May‑to‑Oct period.
  • The monthly freight loading for Indian Railways rose by 8.32 % to 145.07 million tonnes, while passenger traffic hit 64.72 crore (a 4.2 % rise in non‑suburban corridors).
  • The Coal export volume in October 2026 was 0.44 MT (₹3,913.5 crore), down 32.0 % from the previous period, while imports climbed to 0.76 MT (₹8,803.6 crore), a 65.8 % increase over the same horizon.
  • In international trade, India’s majority of petroleum exports accounted for ~45 % of total global trade, and green hydrogen exports are now the largest single off‑take segment globally, even as India’s overall trade surplus expands to reach $489 million.
05

International Affairs & Organizations

India’s diplomacy and multilateral engagement grow in intensity during this week.

  • The India‑EU Free Trade Agreement (FTA) to be signed by 30 Nov 2026 is expected to deepen economic ties between the two blocs.
  • India’s participation in the 27th UN General Assembly was highlighted at a G20 summit held in Bali, where India was the guested country for this edition.
  • The India‑Canada Defence Dialogue (First Meeting) held on 03 Nov 2026 launched a framework for joint-industry defence cooperation and R&D.
  • The India‑Poland Business Forum was held in Warsaw marking bilateral industrial and technology collaborations.
  • The India‑Syria MoU was signed in October 2026 for remote‑controlled drone training for the Syrian Air Defence.
06

Awards, Rankings & Appointments

India’s top performers in quality and public service were rewarded or appointed in this week.

  • Coal Star Rating Awards recognized 50 mines for their five‑star performance.
  • The International Aluminium Initiative awarded Bharat Aluminium Industries (BAIL) a silver badge in a ceremony held on 02 Nov 2026.
  • Kerala’s Chief Secretary was appointed Dr Venkatesh P to lead the new MRU Ballater program on 03 Nov 2026.
  • Shivaji Singh was sworn in as the new Chairman of the National Port Authority on 02 Nov 2026.
07

Sports / Environment / Science & Tech

India’s clean‑energy ambitions and scientific achievements have taken centre stage during this week.

  • The Green Energy Corridors programme invested ₹2 lakh crore in transmission lines and substations, and they will now operate under a single-frequency grid across the five regional grids.
  • The green hydrogen price in India was quoted at ₹279 kg (the lowest globally), while the green ammonia price was ₹49.75 kg, also a record low.
  • The India’s first organic fisheries cluster was declared Soreng, Sikkim under Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana with an investment of ₹375 lakh.
  • The National Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 hosted 400 + exhibitors, 75 000 visitors, 135 country delegates and announced a ₹15 lakh crore investment attraction in the last two years, with 15 GW of hybrid renewable projects auctioned.
