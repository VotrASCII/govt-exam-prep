RBI & Monetary Policy

  • ★ The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) announced a 4-day Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction of ₹50,000 crore under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) on Monday, 23 November 2026. The bidding window was 9:30 AM to 10:00 AM, with reversal on Friday, 27 November 2026. RBI took the decision after reviewing current and evolving near-term liquidity conditions. Operational guidelines are those of Press Release 2021-2022/1572 dated 20 January 2022, so this continues RBI's use of short-tenor VRR operations to fine-tune liquidity.
  • ★ RBI announced a conversion/switch of State Government Securities (SGS) worth ₹10,800 crore (face value), with the auction on Thursday, 26 November 2026. Five States are involved, each with two source securities.
    • Madhya Pradesh: ₹1,000 crore + ₹1,000 crore.
    • Punjab: ₹800 crore + ₹1,000 crore.
    • Rajasthan: ₹1,000 crore + ₹1,000 crore.
    • Tamil Nadu: ₹1,000 crore + ₹2,000 crore.
    • Uttar Pradesh: ₹1,000 crore + ₹1,000 crore.

Short-dated State Development Loans (SDLs) maturing in 2027–2029 are swapped into longer SGS maturing between 2034 and 2044, which helps States lengthen their debt profile. Press Release 2026-2027/1539 covers this.

  • The switch auction is run on e-Kuber, RBI's Core Banking Solution, as a multiple-price auction. Bids are accepted between 10:30 AM and 11:30 AM on 26 November, the result is announced the same day, and settlement is on T+1, i.e. Friday, 27 November 2026. The minimum bid is ₹10,000 and multiples thereafter. The source security must be quoted at the Financial Benchmarks India Pvt. Ltd. (FBIL) closing price of the previous working day, and bids at any other price are rejected.
  • The mechanics of rounding and settlement are exam-friendly details. The switch ratio (source price ÷ destination price) is rounded off at 8 decimal places. The destination security's face value is rounded down to a multiple of ₹10,000. Odd amounts below ₹10,000 are notionally allotted and bought back at the quoted bid price. In RBI's illustration, a ₹10,00,00,000 source security with ratio 0.98286290 gives a destination face value of ₹9,82,80,000, with an odd amount of ₹6,290 and cash consideration of ₹6,240. Cash settlement is only for net accrued interest plus this rounding cash.
  • ★ RBI announced the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills worth ₹23,000 crore on Wednesday, 25 November 2026. The notified amounts are ₹8,000 crore (91-Day), ₹8,000 crore (182-Day) and ₹7,000 crore (364-Day), with settlement on Thursday, 26 November 2026. The auction is price-based using the multiple-price method. Competitive bids are accepted from 10:30 AM to 11:30 AM and non-competitive bids from 10:30 AM to 11:00 AM, through e-Kuber. Retail investors may bid non-competitively via the Retail Direct portal, with their allocation capped at 5 percent of the notified amount.
  • ★ RBI imposed a monetary penalty of ₹7.50 lakh on Fusion Finance Limited by an order dated 17 November 2026. The penalty was imposed under section 58G(1)(b) read with section 58B(5)(aa) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. It followed a statutory inspection based on the company's financial position as on 31 March 2026. Two charges were sustained: failing to obtain PAN, an equivalent e-document or Form 60 while opening account-based relationships under Know Your Customer (KYC) norms, and failing to escalate rejected complaints to the Internal Ombudsman within the prescribed time. RBI clarified that the action is regulatory and does not pronounce on the validity of any customer transaction (Press Release 2026-2027/1537).
  • ★ India's total foreign exchange reserves stood at US$ 705,342 million (₹6,647,812 crore) as on 13 November 2026, per the Weekly Statistical Supplement. Reserves fell by US$ 14,205 million over the week. Foreign Currency Assets were US$ 583,452 million (down US$ 7,897 million) and gold was US$ 98,613 million (down US$ 6,183 million). Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) were US$ 18,517 million and the Reserve Position in the International Monetary Fund (IMF) was US$ 4,758 million.
  • Scheduled commercial bank data as on 31 October 2026 show strong balance-sheet expansion. Aggregate deposits were ₹28,251,076 crore, up 16.9% year-on-year, and bank credit was ₹23,182,499 crore, up 19.5%. Food credit was ₹123,267 crore. The release also notes that under the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025, a "fortnight" now means the 15th and the last calendar day of a month, effective 15 December 2025, replacing alternate Fridays.
  • Broad money (M3) was ₹33,768,416 crore on 31 October 2026, up 16.5% year-on-year. This compares with ₹31,453,126 crore on 31 March 2026. Currency with the public was ₹4,267,896 crore and time deposits with banks were ₹25,738,861 crore. RBI's loans and advances to State Governments were ₹21,004 crore on 13 November 2026, against ₹15,415 crore a week earlier and ₹31,808 crore a year earlier.
  • ★ Payment-system links with partner countries featured in trade talks this week. At the 14th India–Thailand Joint Trade Committee, the sides discussed linking India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) with Thailand's PromptPay, local currency settlement, and FinTech cooperation between RBI and the Bank of Thailand. In Egypt, Piyush Goyal discussed linking UPI with Egypt's Instapay. These steps extend India's cross-border UPI network and its push for local-currency trade settlement.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • ★ Public Sector Banks (PSBs) posted their highest-ever aggregate net profit of ₹1,09,540 crore in H1 FY2026-27 (as on 30.09.2026), up 16.9% year-on-year. Operating profit was ₹1.81 lakh crore, up 13.9%, and Return on Assets was 1.11%, up 3 basis points (bps). Profit has improved for the fifth consecutive year. This is the headline data point for the sector's health in the Financial Stability Report style of questioning.
  • ★ PSB asset quality remains very strong, with Gross NPA at 1.69% and Net NPA at 0.33%. Slippages were 0.62% and recoveries and upgradations were 21.1%. All PSBs have a Provision Coverage Ratio above 90%. The low Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) level continues the multi-year downward trend that examiners track.
  • PSB capital and efficiency indicators improved. Capital to Risk-weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) was 16.89%, up 93 bps, while risk-weighted asset (RWA) density fell 94 bps to 47.9%. The cost-to-income ratio improved 191 bps to 47.38% and cost per asset fell 14 bps to 1.60%.
  • PSB aggregate business crossed ₹300 lakh crore for the first time, at ₹301.6 lakh crore (up 15.5%). Deposits were ₹275 lakh crore (up 13.1%) and advances grew 18.8%. Credit growth was 17% in retail, 19.5% in agriculture and 20.4% in Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME). The release gives two different advances figures (₹127 lakh crore in the headline and ₹136.4 lakh crore in the body), so rely on growth rates for questions.
  • A High-Level Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat has held two sittings. It has circulated questionnaires to banks, NBFCs (Non-Banking Financial Companies), DFIs (Development Finance Institutions), regulators, industry associations and fintechs. It will make recommendations on Banking for Viksit Bharat 2047.
  • The Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (IEPFA), under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, held 'Niveshak Shivir' on 17 November 2026 with SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India) and Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs). The venue was Jacaranda Hall, India Habitat Centre, New Delhi. Services included recovery of unclaimed dividends and shares pending six to seven years, and on-the-spot KYC and nomination updates. New Delhi was the 8th city in a series that began in Pune in 2025, over 310 investors participated, and over 62,700 claims were approved in the last financial year.
  • ★ The SVANidhi Samman Awards 2026 were conferred on 20 November 2026 in New Delhi by Minister Manohar Lal. There were 52 awards and 5 special recognitions across 35 States/UTs, 4,900+ urban local bodies and 120+ lending institutions. State Bank of India (SBI) won for overall loan performance, while Bank of Baroda, Punjab National Bank (PNB) and Union Bank of India were recognised for loan performance. Special recognitions went to FSSAI, ISB, NPCI, Paytm and PhonePe.
  • ★ PM SVANidhi (launched June 2020) has reached about 81 lakh street vendors with 1.21 crore+ loans of about ₹20,000 crore. It was restructured in August 2025, with about 22 lakh loans worth ₹6,294 crore to 10.6 lakh new beneficiaries in the first year. A UPI-linked RuPay Credit Card with a limit up to ₹30,000 is offered to those who repaid the second tranche. The linked 'SVANidhi se Samriddhi' programme profiled 52 lakh+ families, and 50 Street Food Hubs are being developed.
  • Union Home and Cooperation Minister Amit Shah visited the Pune Regional Office of the Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies (CRCS). The CRCS is appointed under Article 243ZH(f) and Section 4(1) of the Multi-State Cooperative Societies (MSCS) Act, 2002. Pune is the first CRCS Regional Office, operating since 15 April 2026 and overseeing 818 MSCS. Nationwide, 1,872 MSCS are registered, 420 of them since the Ministry of Cooperation was established on 6 July 2021. Lucknow, Chennai and Kolkata are planned next.
  • Arunachal Pradesh's cooperative base has 185 PACS (Primary Agricultural Credit Societies), 52 dairy and 43 fisheries cooperative societies, covering 56,000+ people. Shah unveiled 'Arunachal Pradesh Cooperative Vision 2047' at Itanagar on 22 November 2026, under the theme 'Sahkar Se Samriddhi'. Awards included Namsai Dairy Cooperative Society (Cooperation Excellence), Lower Subansiri Fish Farmers Cooperative Society (Best Fisheries), Kemli LAMPS (Best Handloom, Textile and Weavers) and Jang LAMPS (Best PACS/LAMPS). Support included an NCDC loan certificate and a NABARD micro-ATM.
  • The SVAMITVA scheme (launched 24 April 2020) has issued 3.47 crore property cards for 2.20 lakh villages. Drone surveys cover 3.28 lakh of 3.33 lakh target villages (98%). Banks have extended 16,614 loans worth ₹1,769.56 crore against property cards, so this is a tool for formal credit access. The saturation target is 31 March 2027.
  • Women entrepreneurship in MSMEs was highlighted on Women Entrepreneurship Day (19 November 2026). Nearly 38% of MSMEs are women-led and more than 10 crore enterprises are registered. The PMEGP supported 25 lakh+ jobs for women, and public procurement from women-owned micro and small enterprises exceeded the mandatory 3% target. RBI's recognition of women-led self-help groups as reliable borrowers and TReDS (for timely payments) were cited.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

  • ★ The Export Promotion Mission (EPM) has an outlay of ₹25,060 crore for FY 2025-26 to FY 2030-31 and was approved by the Cabinet on 12 November 2025. Amended guidelines issued on 17 November 2026 under the 'Niryat Disha' sub-scheme are the latest revision.
    • Market Access Support: the turnover ceiling for airfare reimbursement was raised from ₹75 lakh to ₹50 crore, and the Export Promotion Council membership period was cut from 6 months to 3 months.
    • TRACE: eligible certificates rose from 462 to 600.
    • LIFT: low export-intensity district coverage expanded from 13 to 34 States/UTs, and the minimum origin–destination distance was cut from 200 km to 100 km.
  • ★ PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana was launched on 13 February 2024 with an outlay of ₹75,021 crore. It is described as the world's largest domestic rooftop solar programme. About 65 lakh households have been solarised, ₹28,024 crore of Central Financial Assistance was disbursed till July 2026, and 21 lakh+ households have zero bills.
  • ★ PM Surya Sarovar Yojana (PM-SSY) was approved on 31 July 2026 for 5,000 MW of floating solar with co-located storage. Storage is at least 2 hours (10,000 MWh). The outlay is ₹5,070 crore for FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31, and the National Institute of Solar Energy (NISE) estimates potential at 102.18 GWp.
  • PM-KUSUM (2019) targets about 34,800 MW by 2026-27 with Central support of ₹34,422 crore. As on 30.09.2026 there were 11.91 lakh off-grid solar pumps and 17.18 lakh feeder-solarised pumps. Related solar-manufacturing support includes a PLI scheme launched in 2021 (₹4,500 crore) and expanded in 2022 (+₹19,500 crore), plus a ₹6,853 crore Viability Gap Funding for 1,000 MW offshore wind.
  • ★ The Small Hydro Power Development Scheme FY 2026-27 to 2030-31 has an outlay of ₹2,584.60 crore and targets about 1,500 MW. Small hydro means up to 25 MW and is administered by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE). Potential is 21,134 MW across 7,133 sites, of which about 5,182 MW (about 25%) is harnessed. North-Eastern States get ₹3.6 crore/MW or 30% of project cost (whichever is lower), capped at ₹30 crore per project.
  • ★ The new Khelo India Scheme was approved by the Union Cabinet with an outlay of ₹36,441 crore for five years. The Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme has an outlay of ₹1,856.67 crore. Union Sports Minister Dr Mansukh Mandaviya announced this at the FICCI TURF summit on 20 November 2026.
  • ★ PM Narendra Modi announced the PM-SETU scheme of ₹60,000 crore to upgrade Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) at the 5th Kaushal Deekshant Samaroh in Lucknow on 21 November 2026. Nearly 5,000 new ITIs have been added since 2014 and 30+ new-age trades introduced. The PM also said WorldSkills Asia will be held in Greater Noida in 2027.
  • ★ Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana–Urban (PMAY-U) 2.0, launched September 2024, aims at 1 crore additional families. The 11th Central Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (CSMC) meeting sanctioned 1.75 lakh houses, with in-principle approval for 1.5 lakh more (Andhra Pradesh 1,27,349; Maharashtra 22,484). Total sanctioned under PMAY-U 2.0 is 24.15 lakh+, and PMAY-U plus 2.0 together show 1.29 crore sanctioned and 1.01 crore completed.
  • The Ministry of Rural Development issued Ombudsperson and Appellate Authority guidelines under the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB-G RAM G). A district-level Ombudsperson handles grievances on job cards, wages and worksite facilities. A digital platform tracks complaints, and Ombudspersons must attend at least three Social Audit hearings or Gram Sabhas every month.
  • ★ Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas (15 November 2026, 151st birth anniversary of Birsa Munda) saw PM Modi inaugurate and lay foundation stones for 246 projects worth about ₹3,000 crore. The main event was at Gadchiroli, Maharashtra. Inaugurations included 38 Eklavya Model Residential Schools and 100 Multi-Purpose Centres under PM-JANMAN, and the PM said over ₹1 lakh crore is allocated for PM-JANMAN and Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan. The day was declared by the Union Cabinet in 2021.
  • The Forest Rights Act, 2006 backgrounder gives progress as on 31 August 2026. About 54 lakh claims were received and 43.56 lakh (80.64%) disposed, with 25.42 lakh titles distributed covering nearly 238 lakh acres. Chhattisgarh leads with 5,34,068 titles, followed by Odisha (4,73,936) and Madhya Pradesh (2,89,461). A National FRA Digital Platform was launched during Janjatiya Gaurav Pakhwada (1–15 November 2026).
  • The Union Budget of February 2026 proposed five Regional Medical Value Tourism Hubs, linked to the Ayush Visa and the Heal in India initiative. The Ayush Visa was introduced on 27 July 2023. Medical value travel patients rose from 1,82,945 (2020) to 6,44,387 (2024).
  • A ₹493 crore Phase-I Telecom Manufacturing Zone at Gwalior was reviewed at a Letter of Intent ceremony on 19 November 2026. It is billed as Asia's first integrated telecom manufacturing cluster. The Centre gives 100% funding, Madhya Pradesh provided about 170 acres free, and the SPV is held 51% by Madhya Pradesh and 49% by the Department of Telecommunications.
  • The Samaveshi Niti Sankalp departmental summit (18–19 November 2026) was the third in the series of departmental summits. It had four themes: Nasha Mukt Bharat, One Nation One Scholarship Portal, Early Intervention and Reduction of Disability Burden, and Sugamya Bharat. Action points include observing the 10th of every month as Nasha Mukti Sankalp Diwas from December 2026 and at least one de-addiction centre in every district by March 2029.

Economy & Trade

  • ★ Wholesale Price Index (WPI) inflation rose to 12.29% in October 2026 (base year 2022-23), from 11.39% in September 2026. The All Commodities index was 113.3 against 112.5. Fuel and Power inflation was 32.84% (index 115.7), Primary Articles 9.24% and Manufactured Products 9.54%. The Food Index (weight 24.99%) was 8.58%.
  • WPI drivers were energy prices. Crude Petroleum and Natural Gas inflation was 70.80%, Mineral Oils 45.57%, Chemicals 17.53% and Basic Metals 17.21%. Cumulative April–October FY 2026-27 inflation is 10.14%, against −0.30% in FY 2025-26. The next release is on Monday, 14 December 2026.
  • ★ The Index of Core Industries (ICI, base 2022-23) grew 10.9% year-on-year in October 2026 (provisional), against 8.2% in September (final). Iron Ore grew 26.4%, Coal 23.1%, Electricity 21.6%, Cement 15.4% and Steel 7.3%. Natural Gas (−7.6%), Crude Oil (−4.1%) and Fertilizers (−12.2%) contracted. Cumulative growth for April–October 2026 was 6.0% versus 2.3% a year earlier.
  • ★ The Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) monthly bulletin for October 2026 shows labour force participation of 55.7% (age 15+) and an unemployment rate of 4.9%. It is the 19th monthly bulletin, using Current Weekly Status. Female participation was 35.0%, the highest since February 2026, and the Worker Population Ratio was 53.0%. Urban unemployment was 6.7% and rural female unemployment 3.8%, the lowest of 2026.
  • ★ India's latest GDP-growth markers are mixed with new statistical series. Amit Shah cited that Moody's raised India's 2026-27 growth forecast from 6.5% to 7%, alongside record exports of USD 863 billion in 2025-26 (target USD 1 trillion). MoSPI (Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation) is moving to new series: GDP and IIP (Index of Industrial Production) with base 2022-23 (GDP released 27 February 2026, IIP on 1 June 2026) and CPI (Consumer Price Index) with base 2024 (released 12 February 2026).
  • The new CPI series is based on the Household Consumption Expenditure Survey (HCES) 2023-24 and COICOP 2018. It covers 358 items including 50 services, with prices from 1,465 rural and 1,395 urban markets in 434 towns plus 12 online markets. Rural house rent is included for the first time. The MoSPI Data Users Conference in Mumbai on 23 November 2026 featured RBI Deputy Governor Dr Poonam Gupta.
  • ★ India's electronics exports in April–September FY 2026-27 rose 44.0% to USD 31.97 billion. Smartphones rose 35.8% to USD 18.17 billion, but their share fell from 60.3% to 56.8%. Non-smartphone electronics rose 56.5% to USD 13.79 billion. Memory ICs rose 2,333% to USD 562.4 million, and mounted PCBs jumped from USD 63.3 million to USD 2.10 billion, with Singapore the top destination.
  • ★ Regional export growth in April–September 2026-27 was led by Africa (+44% to US$ 31.42 billion). Asia rose 21% to US$ 103.84 billion, the Americas 13% to US$ 66.82 billion, Europe 8% to US$ 51.04 billion and CIS and Baltics 7% to US$ 3.36 billion. The USA (US$ 50.80 billion) was the largest single destination, and petroleum products exports to Africa grew 137%.
  • ★ Exports to Free Trade Agreement (FTA) partners surged in April–September 2026-27. ASEAN rose 58% to US$ 28.06 billion (Singapore +103%, Malaysia +83%), Japan +40%, the EU +5% to US$ 38.79 billion, Oman +47% and the UK +7% to US$ 7.32 billion. The South Asian Free Trade Area (SAFTA) bloc rose 22% to US$ 15.29 billion. This is a useful snapshot of how recent agreements are affecting trade.
  • Horticulture Third Advance Estimates 2025-26 put production at 3,869.15 lakh tonnes, up 4.36%. Fruits were 1,230.66 lakh tonnes, vegetables 2,272.68 lakh tonnes and potato 655.70 lakh tonnes (up 11.95%). Area was about 297.21 lakh hectares.
  • India's shipbuilding push targets the top 10 nations by 2030 and top 5 by 2047. The India Shipbuilding Investment Conclave (16 November 2026) projected about USD 10 billion for five greenfield coastal clusters: Dugarajapatnam, Kuchhadi, Dighi, Kendrapada and Thoothukudi. A USD 8 billion financial package is in place, and the order book exceeds 150 vessels worth over USD 1.7 billion.
  • Commercial coal mining continues to expand. The 16th round (with the 2nd attempt of the 15th) drew 85 bids for 18 of 26 blocks, with 20 first-time bidders. Captive and commercial coal production rose 10.22% to 210.46 million tonnes in FY 2025-26.
  • Chennai Port recorded an all-time high of 2.03 lakh tonnes of rice exports to Africa between April and 18 November 2026. Separately, Census 2027, India's first digital Census, begins Phase II (Population Enumeration) with self-enumeration in Goa, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and non-snow-bound Uttarakhand till 30 November 2026.
