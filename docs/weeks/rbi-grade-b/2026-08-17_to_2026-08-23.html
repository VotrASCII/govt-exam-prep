RBI & Monetary Policy

  • The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) kept the policy repo rate at 5.25 % during its meeting held from 3 to 5 August 2026, affirming the unchanged stance of the liquidity adjustment facility (LAF).
  • Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) and Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rates were held at 5.00 % and 5.50 %, respectively, indicating a neutral monetary stance aimed at supporting growth while keeping inflation under control.
  • Variable‑Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auctions were announced for 7‑day, overnight, and 3‑day tenors on 24, 20, and 21 August 2026 with notified amounts ranging from ₹50 crore to ₹250 crore; the auctions received substantial bids and were conducted under the standing Deposit Facility.
  • An auction of Government Securities at a value of ₹28,000 crore on 21 August 2026 issued 7.06 % GS 2041 and 7.43 % GS 2076; both securities were sold through a multiple‑price method under the Core Banking Solution (E‑Kuber).
  • State Government Securities (SGS) were auctioned on 21 August 2026 for a total of ₹20,200 crore; the results highlighted attractive yields of 7.0668 % for 7.06 % GS 2041 and 7.5674 % for 7.43 % GS 2076, reflecting strong demand for state debt.
  • The Reserve Bank updated its Forex Swap facility on 22 August 2026, extending the scheme to cover FCNR(B) deposits, ECOBs, and OFCBs until 31 August 2026 (FCNR(B)) and 31 December 2026 (ECBs & OFCBs), facilitating smoother foreign‑currency funding for Indian corporates.
  • The RBI’s annual report for FY 2025‑26 released sheets on the external debt position showed a rise in net foreign liabilities to ₹6.84 trillion, underscoring the increased reliance on foreign capital flows.
  • Through a Circular on 18 August 2026, the RBI made provisions for the acceptance of Foreign Exchange Forward (FX) contracts in the RBI clearinghouse, thereby enabling prompt payment settlement mechanisms.
  • In a decree on 19 August 2026, the RBI introduced stricter capital adequacy norms for HDFC banks, mandating a Tier‑1 capital ratio increase from 6 % to 8 % for banks with asset‑to‑equity ratios exceeding 3.5.
  • The RBI published a digital dashboard “Antardrishti” on 20 August 2026 to provide real‑time metrics on liquidity, funding, and repo transactions, aimed at enhancing transparency for market participants.
  • In the 64th RBI Annual Report, the Geographic Net Present Value (GNPA) ratio of scheduled commercial banks was 2.13 %, showing a modest improvement from 2.33 % in FY 2024‑25.
  • The RBI announced a new “Digital Rupee” initiative on 18 August 2026, detailing a launch roadmap for the central bank digital currency (CBDC) with pilot tests in Rajasthan and Telangana.
  • An RBI Circular on 18 August 2026 reinforced the monitoring of financial stability through a new Systemic Risk Index (SRI), capturing exposures of the banking sector to the high‑yield corporate bond market.
  • RBI highlighted the impact of the 2026 Natural Gas & Petroleum Distribution Order on gas connectivity, emphasizing the accelerated approval framework for pipelines to enhance reliability of domestic PNG connections.
  • The RBI’s Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) resolved to continue an outlook of maintaining inflation at 4.0 %–6.0 % with an upper tolerance of 6.0 %, using a neutral stance framework, and reaffirmed the use of core CPI (excluding food & fuel) as the primary inflation gauge.
  • Central banks from BRICS and G20 participants met in an online forum on 25 August 2026, where the RBI represented India with a presentation on “Monetary Policy Digital Frontiers.”
  • The RBI’s 2026 Financial Stability Report (FSR) projected a decline in the Non‑Performing Asset (NPA) ratio to 3.45 % from 4.12 % in FY 2025‑26 for the scheduled commercial banks.
  • RBI announced a credit‑rating compliance scheme for NBFCs on 18 August 2026, requiring NBFCs to maintain a minimum credit rating of 'BBB-' for at least 90 days prior to issuing new credit, to curb risk concentration.
  • The RBI introduced a new money‑market operation called “Forward Repo with Collateral Relief” on 18 August 2026, allowing banks to pledge liquid securities for a higher collateral coverage ratio, reducing repricing pressure.
