RBI Grade B · Week 37 · General Awareness

28 Sep – 04 Oct, 2026

13 topics summarised · 0 practice questions

01

RBI & Monetary Policy

  • Rabi 2026 fertiliser subsidy – The Union Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare announced a total outlay of 2 lakh crore for fertiliser subsidies, comprising Urea at ₹266 per bag and DAP at ₹1,350 per bag. This figure is a key indicator of the government’s support for agriculture and remains a perennial high‑priority topic for the RBI Annual Report and Budget statistics.
  • Fastag annual pass pricing – The Ministry of Finance launched a one‑year Fastag pass priced at ₹3,075, valid for up to 1,150 fee plazas. This milestone is exam‑relevant because it reflects the RBI’s Payment Systems agenda, especially UPI and NEFT/RTGS payment reach.
  • Fastag passes issued – In 2025‑26, Fastag has issued 1 crore passes, signalling a 1% penetration in the 1,100 M vehicle cohort. The RBI cites this as a success metric for digital payments.
  • Integrated aluminium complex investment – India and UAE jointly committed US$ 11.5 billion to a new aluminium complex in Odisha, part of the larger $400 billion Indo‑UAE trade vision. RBI’s view on industrial investment is a recurring POA in Monetary Policy.
  • Target trade volume India‑UAE – The bilateral trade goal is US$ 200 billion by 2032, reflecting India’s growing export and import corridors. Tied to RBI’s Trade Policy review.
  • Rabi 2026 procurement horizon – The Ministry of Agriculture announced a procurement window for the “Khet Bachao Abhiyan” from 22 Oct to 30 Nov 2026 to align with state‑level procurement.
  • Green‑highways plantation – In the last five years, the Ministry of Road Transport & Highways planted 3.61 crore saplings along 1.32 lakh km of National Highways, a national figure used in RBI ESG and green finance metrics.
02

Banking & Financial Sector

  • RBI Circulars on UCBs – In its latest policy bulletin, the RBI reiterated the exposure limits of Ultra‑Credit‑Batteries (UCBs) at 100 % of the total branch exposure, and extended the ceiling to 75 % for “premium” UCBs, a key regulatory change for RBI & monetary‑policy outlook.
  • NBFC Regulations – RBI issued new guidelines requiring all NBFCs to obtain a credit‑rating of AAA from the four major rating agencies; non‑compliance triggers a penalty of ₹5 lakh.
  • HFC Deposit Acceptances – RBI capped deposits held by Home Finance Companies at 2% of their loan book, to shield them from liquidity shocks that could impact the broader financial sector.
  • Payment Systems Update – RBI’s Digital Rupee pilot is now operating in Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh, with daily transaction volume exceeding ₹150 crore, reflecting the core Payment System developments.
  • Antardrishti Dashboard – The RBI’s new risk‑monitoring dashboard (Antardrishti) was updated this week, adding a “Money Kumar” mascot to display the health of selected NBFCs, thereby enhancing transparency for investors.
03

Government Schemes & Budget

  • Rabi 2026 fertiliser plan – The Ministry announced ₹2 lakh crore subsidies for fertilisers, with subsidies per bag: Urea ₹266, DAP ₹1,350, a major fiscal stimulus included in the Union Budget 2026 ‑ 27.
  • PM AJAY – The PM Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana released a community‑level breakdown: 47,243 villages served, 785 student hostels completed, and an allocation of ₹61.46 crore in FY 2026‑27 to sponsor two hostels, a protection programme for low‑income groups.
  • PM E‑DRIVE – With an outlay of ₹10,900 crore, the scheme aims to test EV‑batteries and power‑trains in line with the national green‑mobility quota. It was announced during the annual symposium hosted by the Minister of Science & Technology.
  • Fastag milestone – The Ministry of Finance declared the issuance of 1 crore passes, encompassing 1,150 toll plazas. This aligns with the new “Digital Payment” mandate in the Budget.
  • Special Campaign 6 – Three ministries (Commerce & Industry, Environment, Rural Development) launched “Special Campaign 6” to clean public offices, dispose e‑waste and manage pending paperwork, with an expected clearance of ₹5.1 crore from scrappage.
04

Economy & Trade

  • Exports of petroleum products – The Department of Commerce reported that petroleum and petroleum products remain the largest exporter at 30% of India’s total merchandise export value, reflecting a growth‑driven sector.
  • Rabi 2026 production rise – Rabi 2026 food‑grain production escalated by 205 lakh tonnes between 2023‑24 and 2025‑26, hitting a peak of 84 lakh tonnes from the historical 40 lakh tonnes.
  • Trade deal agenda – The High‑Level Joint Task Force with UAE is on track to output a 200 billion US$ trade pact by 2032 in addition to a comprehensive CEPA, signalling a shift in India’s trade diplomacy.
  • FTAs – India has signed FTAs with 21 countries, although a FTA with Brazil remains pending, a distinction often tested in current‑affairs queries.
05

International Affairs & Organizations

  • G20 Trade Ministers’ Meeting – On 30 Sep 26 and 1 Oct 26 in Milwaukee, India presented a new set of trade‑policy proposals focusing on SMEs and agrarian resilience, underlining its role in the G20 agenda.
  • India–UAE Joint Task Force – Co‑chairs are Piyush Goyal and Sheikh Hamed bin Zayed, meeting on 28 Sep 26 in Mumbai, looking at a US$ 3 billion investment in the Emirates NBD‑RBL tie‑up.
  • India–Ethiopia Joint Defence Cooperation – A second JDC meeting in Addis Ababa reaffirmed the 2025 defence MoU, increasing defense collaboration to a strategic partnership, a key point in India’s security diplomacy.
  • India–Kazakhstan KAZIND‑2026 – A 60‑member Indian contingent joined the counter‑terrorism exercise in Oskemen, marking India’s deeper military cooperation in Central Asia.
06

Awards, Rankings & Appointments

  • NIRF 2026 – IISc Bengaluru secured the top position in the NIRF rankings for the 2026 cycle, and IIT Madras re‑claimed the 3rd spot, marking the country’s sustained excellence in higher education.
  • Brand Finance India 2026 – The rank‑ordering of India’s consumer brands highlighted Reliance and Tata as the top two, a benchmark for brand equity analysts.
07

Sports / Environment / Science & Tech

  • Asian Games 2026 – Medals – Indian athletes won 7 medals: 2 golds (women’s hockey and women’s 25 m pistol), 3 silvers (men’s 1500 m, women's 400 m hurdles, javelin), and 2 bronzes (women’s 5000 m, men's long‑jump).
  • Fastag digital drives – 1‑crore annual passes streamlined ≈ 1,150 fee plazas, a public‑service initiative improving transit payment ecosystems.
  • Green high‑ways – 3.61 crore saplings were planted along 1.32 lakh km of highways, with a 25% increase in plantation coverage relative to 2023.
  • Digital Rupee pilot – The payment service is running in Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh, recording a daily volume of ₹150 crore.
