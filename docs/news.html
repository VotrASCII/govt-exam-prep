8th Pay Commission: MSA and IRTSA proposals show how fitment factors could reshape central government salary structures

Two employee bodies, MSA and IRTSA, have submitted contrasting salary proposals to the 8th Pay Commission, differing in the fitment factor they want and the resulting basic pay.
