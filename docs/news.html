Missed SIP installment? Know the impact on your bank account, CIBIL score and investments

The costs are indirect: your bank may charge a return or penalty fee for the bounced mandate, and several consecutive failures can lead the fund house to cancel the SIP. For exams, remember that CIBIL scores track credit repayment behaviour, not investment instalments.
