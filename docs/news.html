8th Pay Commission: Check how ₹25,000, ₹35,000 and ₹50,000 basic salaries could change under proposed fitment factors

Employee bodies have pushed for fitment factors of 3.61, 3.833 and 4.00 for the 8th Pay Commission, which would revise central government pay. The fitment factor is the multiplier applied to existing basic pay to get the new basic, and the 7th Pay Commission used 2.57. Under these proposals a ₹25,000 basic becomes about ₹90,250, ₹95,825 or ₹1,00,000, and a ₹35,000 basic becomes about ₹1,26,350, ₹1,34,155 or ₹1,40,000. A ₹50,000 basic becomes about ₹1,80,500, ₹1,91,650 or ₹2,00,000. These are demands, not a final decision. The factor ultimately adopted will shape the government's wage bill and fiscal deficit.

Centre hikes windfall tax on diesel, ATF exports; petrol levy unchanged — Check details

The Centre has raised the windfall tax on exports of diesel and aviation turbine fuel (ATF) and left the petrol levy unchanged. The previous revision, on October 31, raised the diesel export levy from ₹8.5 to ₹11.5 per litre and the ATF levy from ₹10.5 to ₹11 per litre, and scrapped the ₹2.5 per litre levy on petrol exports. Windfall taxes are periodic export duties that capture extra profits refiners earn when global fuel margins are high. They are a recurring topic on fiscal policy and energy trade.
